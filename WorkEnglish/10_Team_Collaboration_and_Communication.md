# Topic 10: Team Collaboration & Communication (团队协作与沟通)

This guide covers key English vocabulary, phrases, and a sample dialogue for general teamwork, feedback, and communication in a professional environment.

---

## 2. Common Phrases (常用短语)

**Scheduling & Meetings (安排与会议)**
*   "Are you free for a quick sync?" (你有空快速沟通一下吗？)
*   "Let's schedule a meeting to discuss this in detail." (让我们安排个会议详细讨论这个。)
*   "Can we move this conversation to Slack?" (我们可以转到 Slack 上讨论这个吗？)
*   "I have a hard stop at 3 PM." (我下午3点必须结束/有事。)

**Collaboration (协作)**
*   "I can help you with that if you are stuck." (如果你卡住了，我可以帮你。)
*   "Let's pair program on this feature." (让我们结对编程做这个功能。)
*   "Could you share your screen?" (你能共享屏幕吗？)
*   "I'll take the lead on this task." (这个任务由我来带头。)

**Soft Skills & Feedback (软技能与反馈)**
*   "I see your point, but have you considered...?" (我明白你的意思，但你考虑过...吗？)
*   "Thanks for the constructive feedback." (谢谢你建设性的反馈。)
*   "I think there is a misunderstanding." (我觉得这里有个误会。)
*   "Let's agree to disagree and move on." (让我们保留各自意见，继续往下进行。)

## 3. Sample Dialogue (情景对话)

**Context:** Resolving a disagreement on implementation.
**Participants:**
*   **Colleague:** (同事)
*   **You:** Frontend Developer (你)

**Colleague:** "I noticed you refactored the API service layer. But now it looks completely different. I'm finding it hard to understand."

**You:** "Hey, thanks for bringing it up. I wanted to make it more generic so we can reuse it for other modules. **I'm sorry if it caused confusion.**"

**Colleague:** "I get the intention, but it feels a bit over-engineered for our current needs. It takes me twice as long to add a new endpoint now."

**You:** "I see. **I appreciate your feedback.** Maybe I made it too abstract. **Would you be open to a quick call?** I can walk you through my thought process, and maybe we can find a middle ground."

**Colleague:** "Sure, let's sync."

*(On the call)*

**You:** "...So that was my idea. But hearing your concern about complexity, **I agree we should simplify it.** How about we keep the base class but remove the interceptor logic for now?"

**Colleague:** "Yes, that sounds much better. It keeps the reusability but lowers the learning curve. Thanks for being flexible."

**You:** "No problem. **Team alignment is important.** I'll update the code and the documentation."
