# Week 4 Day 1 - Comprehensive Review (Part 1)

> Today: Review **Week 1 and Week 2** core questions (Q1-Q15)
>
> 60 min | Goal: Test yourself on all questions from the first two weeks without notes

---

## Review Checklist

Go through each question below. For each one:
1. Cover the answer section with your hand
2. Try to answer in English (1-2 minutes per question)
3. Record yourself
4. Then check: Did you hit the key points?

---

## Quick Review: Week 1 Questions

### Q: Tell me about yourself (W1D1)
**Key points to hit:**
- Your current role (frontend team lead, 10+ years)
- 1-2 highlights (team size, core projects)
- Why you're here (looking for new challenges)

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I'm currently working as..."
- "I'm responsible for..."
- "I'm looking for..."

**Self-check:** Did I stay under 2 minutes? Did I follow "Present - Past - Future"?

---

### Q: What is your greatest strength? (W1D2)
**Key points to hit:**
- Name one clear strength
- Give a specific example
- Show the result

**Must-use phrases:**
- "My greatest strength is..."
- "For example, I..."
- "This led to / resulted in..."

**Self-check:** Did I give a real example, not just a label?

---

### Q: What is your greatest weakness? (W1D2)
**Key points to hit:**
- Choose a safe weakness
- Acknowledge it honestly
- Show what you're doing to improve

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I sometimes tend to..."
- "I'm aware of this, and I've been working on..."

**Self-check:** Did I avoid saying "I have no weakness"? Did I mention improvement?

---

### Q: Give me examples of good performance (W1D3)
**Key points to hit:**
- Use STAR: Situation - Action - Result
- Include specific outcomes (numbers, impact)
- Show initiative

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I took the initiative to..."
- "This improved / streamlined / reduced..."
- "As a result..."

**Self-check:** Did I mention concrete results?

---

### Q: Why should we hire you? (W1D3)
**Key points to hit:**
- Skills match the job
- Experience proves you can deliver
- Extra value you bring (learning, attitude)

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I believe I'm a strong fit because..."
- "I bring [X] to the table."

**Self-check:** Did I connect my strengths to their needs?

---

### Q: Why do you want to work for our company? (W1D4)
**Key points to hit:**
- Show you researched the company
- Say what attracts you specifically
- Connect to your career goals

**Must-use phrases:**
- "What attracted me was..."
- "I think my skills align well with..."
- "I'm excited about the opportunity to..."

**Self-check:** Did my answer sound specific or generic?

---

## Quick Review: Week 2 Questions

### Q: Tell me about your working experience (W2D1)
**Key points to hit:**
- Overview: years, tech, roles
- Don't list every company - summarize
- Mention your progression

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I have over [X] years of experience..."
- "Throughout my career..."
- "I transitioned into..."

**Self-check:** Was it under 2 minutes? Did I mention key milestones?

---

### Q: Describe a challenging project (W2D1)
**Key points to hit:**
- What the project was (1 sentence)
- What made it challenging (1 sentence)
- How you solved it (2-3 sentences)
- The result (1 sentence)

**Must-use phrases:**
- "One of the most challenging projects was..."
- "To tackle this, I..."
- "In the end..."

**Self-check:** Did I tell a clear story with beginning, middle, and end?

---

### Q: Describe a challenging situation at work (W2D2)
**Key points to hit:**
- Specific situation (urgent tasks, tight deadlines)
- Your action plan
- Positive outcome

**Must-use phrases:**
- "There was a time when..."
- "The first thing I did was..."
- "Despite the pressure..."

**Self-check:** Did I use STAR structure?

---

### Q: How do you handle stress and pressure? (W2D2)
**Key points to hit:**
- You have a method (planning, prioritizing)
- You communicate with your team
- You stay calm

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I start by creating a detailed plan."
- "I prioritize tasks..."
- "I keep my team updated..."

**Self-check:** Did I sound like someone who handles pressure well?

---

### Q: What new tech are you learning? (W2D3)
**Key points to hit:**
- What you're learning (AI, Transformer, etc.)
- Why it matters
- How it connects to your work

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I've been exploring..."
- "I believe [tech] will..."
- "This helps me stay relevant..."

**Self-check:** Did I connect learning to the job?

---

### Q: Why are you qualified? (W2D3)
**Key points to hit:**
- Technical skills match
- Leadership experience
- Learning mindset

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I'm well-suited because..."
- "I have hands-on experience with..."
- "I have a proven track record of..."

**Self-check:** Did I sound confident?

---

### Q: Difficult team member (W2D4)
**Key points to hit:**
- Describe situation neutrally (no blaming)
- Show empathy and communication
- Positive outcome

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I tried to understand their perspective."
- "I suggested / offered to..."
- "After that, our teamwork improved."

**Self-check:** Did I avoid badmouthing anyone?

---

### Q: How do you handle conflicts? (W2D4)
**Key points to hit:**
- Listen first
- Communicate calmly
- Find compromise
- Focus on the issue, not the person

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I first listen to understand..."
- "I focus on the issue, not the person."
- "We found a compromise."

**Self-check:** Did I mention all 4 steps?

## Rapid Fire Practice

Set a timer. Answer each question in 60 seconds or less. Don't stop, don't restart.

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. What's your greatest strength?
3. What's your weakness?
4. Give me an example of good performance.
5. Why should we hire you?
6. Tell me about a challenging project.
7. How do you handle stress?

**After all 7:** Listen to your recordings. Score yourself 1-5 on:
- Fluency (did I keep talking?)
- Structure (was my answer organized?)
- Content (did I hit the key points?)
