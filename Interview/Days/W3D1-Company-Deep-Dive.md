## Useful Expressions

### Showing You Did Your Research
- **I've looked into your company and...**
  - I've looked into your company and I'm really impressed.
- **From what I've read/seen...**
  - From what I've read, your company focuses on innovative solutions.
- **I noticed that your company...**
  - I noticed that your company values open communication.
- **I was interested to learn that...**
  - I was interested to learn that you work with international clients.

### Expressing What Attracted You
- **What attracted me most was...**
  - What attracted me most was your modern tech stack.
- **I'm particularly drawn to...**
  - I'm particularly drawn to your company culture.
- **What really stands out to me is...**
  - What really stands out to me is your focus on innovation.

### Connecting to Your Goals
- **This aligns with my goal of...**
  - This aligns with my goal of working on challenging projects.
- **I believe I can contribute to...**
  - I believe I can contribute to your team's success.
- **I see this as a great opportunity to...**
  - I see this as a great opportunity to grow as a developer.

## The Three Questions Are Really One

Q23, Q24, Q25 are essentially the same question from different angles. Prepare ONE set of material, then adjust:

| Question | Focus Your Answer On |
|----------|---------------------|
| How much do you know? | Facts: what the company does, products, culture |
| Why do you want to work here? | Match: how company fits your career goals |
| What attracted you? | Emotion: what excites you about the company |

## Answer Framework: Know - Like - Fit

```
Know (what you know about the company):
  "I know that your company [facts about company]."
  "You focus on [business area] and use [tech stack]."

Like (what attracts you):
  "What attracted me is [specific thing]."
  "I'm impressed by [achievement/culture/product]."

Fit (how you match):
  "I think my [skills/goals] align well with your [needs/direction]."
  "I believe I can contribute by [how you can help]."
```

## Reference Answers

### Q: How much do you know about our company?

> I've done some research on your company. I know you're a **leader** in developing **user-friendly** web applications. You work with **international clients** and use **modern technologies** like React and TypeScript.
>
> I've also read about some of your **successful projects** on your website. I was **impressed by** the quality and **scale** of your work.
>
> I also understand that your company values **open communication** and **teamwork**, which is something I really **appreciate** in a workplace.

Notes:
- "I've done some research" - 展示你做了功课
- "leader in" - 形容公司地位的好搭配
- "scale of your work" = 工作的规模, 比 "big projects" 更专业

### Q: Why do you want to work for this company?

> A few things really **appeal to** me about your company.
>
> **First**, your tech stack is very **aligned** with my skills. I have years of experience with React and TypeScript, so I think I can **hit the ground running** and contribute **from day one**.
>
> **Second**, I value working in a **collaborative** environment with **open communication**. From what I've seen, that's exactly the kind of culture you have here.
>
> **Third**, I'm always looking to **grow** and take on new **challenges**. Your company seems to offer **diverse** and **interesting** projects, which is exciting to me.

Notes:
- "appeal to me" = 吸引我, 比 "I like" 更正式
- "hit the ground running" = 快速上手, 面试经典习语
- "from day one" = 从第一天起

### Q: What attracted you to our company?

> What **attracted** me most was your **reputation** for quality and innovation. I've seen your project **portfolio**, and the work you do is really **impressive**.
>
> I was also drawn to your **company culture**. I believe that the best work happens when people can communicate **openly** and **honestly** with each other. That's the kind of environment where I **thrive**.
>
> **Plus**, your focus on using **modern technologies** and encouraging **continuous learning** really **resonates** with me. I'm someone who loves to keep learning and improving.

Notes:
- "thrive" = 茁壮成长/表现出色, 高级词汇
- "resonates with me" = 和我产生共鸣, 表达认同感

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Problem | Correct |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Your company is very good. | 太空洞 | Your company has a great **reputation for innovation**. |
| I want money. | 太直接 | I'm looking for a role with **growth opportunities**. |
| I choose because it's famous. | 不够具体 | I chose your company because **your projects align with my skills**. |
| I want to learn from you. | 只谈索取 | I want to **contribute and** keep growing. |

## Expression Upgrades

| Basic | Better | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| I like your company. | I'm genuinely excited about what your company does. | 更有热情 |
| Your tech is good. | Your tech stack aligns well with my expertise. | 更专业 |
| I want to learn. | I'm eager to grow alongside a talented team. | 更有诚意 |
| I can work here. | I'm confident I can contribute from day one. | 更有冲击力 |

## Practice Tasks

### Task 1: Company Research (10 min)
Pick a company you're interested in. Spend 10 min on their website. Fill in the preparation template above.

### Task 2: Assemble Your Answer (10 min)
Using the template, build a "Know - Like - Fit" answer. Record yourself.

### Task 3: No Notes (5 min)
Put away all materials. Answer "Why do you want to work here?" from memory. It's OK to be imperfect.
