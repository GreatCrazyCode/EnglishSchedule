## Part 1: Salary Expectations

### Key Rules
1. **Give a range**, not a specific number
2. **Do your market research** before the interview
3. Show you're **flexible** and consider the whole package
4. If possible, **delay** the conversation until you know more about the role

### Useful Expressions

- **Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking for...**
  - Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking for 20-30k per month.
- **I'd prefer to discuss salary after learning more about the role.**
- **I'm open to discussion on this.**
- **I'd also consider the overall compensation package, including benefits.**
- **I'm flexible, and I'm sure we can find a number that works for both sides.**

### Reference Answer

> I'd prefer to **discuss salary** after learning more about the **specific role** and responsibilities. But **based on** my experience and the **market rate** for this type of position, I'm looking for a salary in the **range of** 20k to 30k per month.
>
> That said, I'm **open to discussion**. Salary is important, but I also **consider** the overall **compensation package** — things like benefits, learning opportunities, and room for growth.

Notes:
- "I'd prefer to discuss later" - 展示你更关心工作内容而非钱
- "market rate" = 市场价, 表示你做了调研
- "compensation package" = 整体薪酬方案, 更专业

## Part 2: Questions for the Interviewer

### Why This Question Matters
- It shows you're **genuinely interested** in the role
- It shows you're **evaluating the company too** (not just being evaluated)
- Prepare **2-3 good questions** before the interview

### Great Questions to Ask

**About the Team:**
- What does the team I'd be working with look like?
- How many frontend developers are on the team?
- Who would I be reporting to?

**About the Work:**
- What kind of projects would I be working on?
- What's the tech stack for the current main project?
- What's the biggest challenge the team is facing right now?

**About Culture:**
- How does the team handle code reviews?
- How does the company support professional development?
- What's the work-life balance like here?

**About Growth:**
- What does career growth look like for this role?
- Are there opportunities to take on leadership responsibilities?

### Questions NOT to Ask (in first interview)
- "How many vacation days do I get?" (wait for offer stage)
- "Can I work from home every day?" (too early)
- "What's the salary?" (let them bring it up)

### Reference Answer

> Yes, I do have a couple of questions.
>
> **First**, I'd love to know more about the **team** I'd be working with. How many developers are on the frontend team, and what's the **current main project**?
>
> **Also**, I'm curious about how your team handles the **balance** between trying out **new technologies** and maintaining **stable, reliable** systems. I think that's always an interesting challenge in engineering.

Notes:
- "I'd love to know" - 比 "I want to know" 更礼貌
- "I'm curious about" - 展示好奇心
- 第二个问题展示了你的技术思考, 让面试官印象深刻

## Part 3: Handling Criticism

### Key Rules
1. Show you're **open to feedback** (not defensive)
2. Describe a **process**: listen, think, improve
3. Frame criticism as a **growth opportunity**
4. Don't say "I'm always right" or "I never get criticized"

### Useful Expressions

- **I welcome constructive criticism.**
- **I try to see feedback as a chance to grow.**
- **I listen carefully to understand the feedback.**
- **I don't take it personally.**
- **I ask for clarification if I'm not sure what to improve.**
- **I appreciate honest feedback because it helps me get better.**

### Reference Answer

> I actually **welcome** constructive criticism. I believe it's one of the best ways to **grow**.
>
> When I receive feedback, I **first listen carefully** to make sure I **fully understand** what's being said. If something isn't clear, I'll **ask questions** to get more details.
>
> Then I **take some time** to think about it. I try **not to take it personally** — instead, I focus on what I can **learn** from it and how I can **improve**.
>
> I think being **open to feedback** is really important, especially in a team environment. Nobody's perfect, and honest feedback helps us all **do better work**.

Notes:
- "I actually welcome" - "actually" 加强语气, 表示你真的欢迎
- "fully understand" - 比 "understand" 更认真
- "take some time to think" - 展示你不会冲动反应
- 结尾从个人上升到团队, 展示大局观

## Combined Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Problem | Correct |
|-------|---------|---------|
| I want 25k money. | 太直接 | I'm looking for a salary in the **range of** 20-30k. |
| I have not question. | 语法错误 | I **don't have any** questions right now. |
| I don't like criticism. | 太负面 | I **welcome** constructive feedback. |
| My salary expect is... | 词性错误 | My salary **expectation** is... |
| I will ask repeat. | 表达不完整 | I'll ask them to **repeat** or **clarify**. |
| I take criticism personal. | 词性错误 | I try not to take it **personally**. |

## Practice Tasks

### Task 1: Salary Script (5 min)
Practice saying your salary range out loud 5 times:
"Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking for a salary in the range of 20 to 30k per month."
- Does it sound natural? Not nervous?

### Task 2: Prepare 3 Questions (10 min)
Write down 3 questions you'd actually want to ask a company. Practice saying them.

### Task 3: Criticism Response (5 min)
Practice the 4-step process:
1. "I listen carefully."
2. "I ask for clarification if needed."
3. "I think about how to improve."
4. "I see it as a chance to grow."
