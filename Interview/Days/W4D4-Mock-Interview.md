## Before You Start

### Preparation Checklist
- [ ] Find a quiet place
- [ ] Have water nearby

- [ ] Turn off distractions
- [ ] Have your phone ready to record

### Mindset Reminders
1. **You've prepared for 3 weeks.** You know these answers.
2. **Fluency over perfection.** Keep talking, don't freeze.
3. **Be yourself.** Interviewers can tell when you're being fake.
4. **It's OK to pause.** Use filler phrases: "That's a great question..."
5. **Smile.** Even on a phone interview, smiling changes your voice.

## The Mock Interview

Use the AI prompt below to run a full simulated interview. If you don't have access to AI, have a friend read the questions, or record yourself answering them in order.

### Interview Structure (30 minutes)

**Phase 1: Small Talk (2-3 min)**
- Greeting
- How are you doing today?
- Did you find the office OK? / How's the weather?

**Phase 2: Background (8-10 min)**
- Tell me about yourself
- Tell me about your current role
- Walk me through your work experience

**Phase 3: Technical/Behavioral (10-12 min)**
- Describe a challenging project you worked on
- How do you handle stress and pressure?
- What new technologies are you learning?
- Have you ever had a conflict with a colleague?

**Phase 4: Company and Career (5-7 min)**
- Why do you want to work here?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
- Why did you leave your last job?

**Phase 5: Practical (3-5 min)**
- What are your salary expectations?
- Do you have any questions for us?

## Small Talk Preparation

Small talk at the beginning sets the tone. Keep it light and friendly.

### Common Small Talk Topics and Responses

**"How are you today?"**
> I'm doing great, thank you. A little nervous actually, but excited to be here!

**"Did you have any trouble finding us?"**
> No, it was easy to find. I looked it up beforehand.

**"How's the weather out there?"**
> It's a beautiful day! Perfect weather to be out.

**"Would you like some water or coffee?"**
> Water would be great, thank you.

**Tip:** Small talk should be 2-3 exchanges max. Keep it short, warm, and natural. Smile!

## Common Interview Body Language Tips

Even for video/phone interviews, these matter:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Sit up straight | Slouch or lean back |
| Make eye contact (look at camera for video) | Look down at notes the whole time |
| Nod when listening | Stay completely still |
| Smile naturally | Have a blank face |
| Use hand gestures moderately | Cross your arms |
| Speak at a moderate pace | Rush through answers |

## Complete AI Mock Interview Prompt

```
You are an interviewer at a foreign tech company in Chengdu. You are interviewing me for a Senior Frontend Developer position.

Conduct a complete 30-minute interview following this structure:

PHASE 1 - Small Talk (2 min):
- Greet me warmly
- Ask a casual question (weather, how I'm doing)
- Transition naturally to the interview

PHASE 2 - Background (8 min):
- "Can you tell me about yourself?"
- "Tell me more about your current role."
- "Can you walk me through your experience?"
(Ask natural follow-up questions based on my answers)

PHASE 3 - Behavioral/Technical (10 min):
- "Describe a challenging project you worked on."
- "How do you handle stress and tight deadlines?"
- "What new technologies have you been learning?"
- "Tell me about a time you had a disagreement with a colleague."
(React naturally - say "I see", "That's interesting", ask follow-ups)

PHASE 4 - Company and Career (5 min):
- "Why are you interested in our company?"
- "Where do you see yourself in five years?"
- "Why did you leave your previous company?"

PHASE 5 - Practical (5 min):
- "What are your salary expectations?"
- "Do you have any questions for us?"
- Close the interview warmly

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Act like a real interviewer - be conversational, not robotic
- React to my answers (nod, say "I see", ask follow-ups)
- Use simple, conversational English
- If I struggle, gently help me along (but note it in evaluation)
- DON'T give feedback during the interview - save it for the end

AFTER THE INTERVIEW, give me a detailed evaluation:
1. Overall impression (1-10)
2. Fluency (1-10)
3. Content quality (1-10)
4. Confidence level (1-10)
5. Top 3 things I did well
6. Top 3 things to improve
7. Which answer was strongest? Why?
8. Which answer was weakest? How to fix it?
9. Specific sentences I should practice more
10. Am I ready for a real interview? (Yes / Almost / Not yet)

Use Chinese for the evaluation section to ensure I fully understand.
```

> show your skills and experience, and be someone the interviewer
> wants to work with. You've prepared well. Trust yourself.
