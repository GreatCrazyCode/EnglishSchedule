# Week 1 · Day 1 — 自我介绍

> 今日面试题：**Can you tell me about yourself?** / **Tell me about your current job**
>
> 学习时间：60 分钟 | 核心目标：学会用简单英语介绍自己的职业背景

---

## 二、实用表达句式

### 开场（说你是谁）
- **I'm a [职位] with [X] years of experience in [领域].**
  - I'm a frontend developer with over 10 years of experience in web development.
- **I've been working as a [职位] for [时间].**
  - I've been working as a frontend team lead for about 3 years.

### 描述当前工作
- **In my current role, I [做什么].**
  - In my current role, I lead a team of 9 frontend developers.
- **I'm responsible for [具体职责].**
  - I'm responsible for work assignment and overcoming technical challenges.
- **My day-to-day work involves [日常内容].**
  - My day-to-day work involves code reviews, mentoring, and building core features.

### 技术背景
- **I mainly work with [技术栈].**
  - I mainly work with React and Vue.
- **I have hands-on experience with [技术].**
  - I have hands-on experience with Three.js and data visualization.

### 过渡到为什么来面试
- **I'm looking for [什么样的机会].**
  - I'm looking for a new challenge where I can keep growing.
- **That's why I'm excited about this opportunity.**
  - Your company's tech stack really matches my skills. That's why I'm excited about this opportunity.

## 三、回答框架

自我介绍遵循 **"现在 → 过去 → 未来"** 三段式：

```
第一段：现在（你是谁，在做什么）
  → "Hi, I'm [名字]. I'm currently a [职位] at [公司]."
  → "I've been in this role for [时间], mainly [做什么]."

第二段：过去（关键经验/亮点，选 1-2 个）
  → "Before that, I worked at [公司], where I [做了什么]."
  → "One thing I'm proud of is [一个成就]."

第三段：未来（为什么来面试）
  → "Now I'm looking for [什么]. I think this role is a great fit because [原因]."
```

## 四、参考回答（带详细注解）

### Q: Can you tell me about yourself?

> Hi, I'm [名字]. I'm a frontend developer with over 10 years of experience.
>
> **Currently**, I'm working as a frontend team lead. I **lead** a team of 9 developers. I'm **responsible for** work assignment, mentoring team members, and solving technical challenges. I **mainly work with** React and Vue.
>
> One thing I'm **proud of** is that I developed a 3D rendering module using Three.js. I also built some tools to **improve** our team's workflow — for example, I wrote a Node.js script to automate our permission management, which **saved** us a lot of time.
>
> Now I'm **looking for** a new challenge. I want to keep **growing** as a developer, and I think this role is a **great fit** for my skills and experience.

**注解：**
- "currently" 放句首，清晰标记时间线
- "responsible for" 后面接名词或动名词（-ing 形式）
- "proud of" 引出你的亮点，比 "I did something good" 更自然
- "looking for" 表示你在寻找，语气积极主动

### Q: Tell me about your current job

> In my current role, I'm a frontend team lead. I **manage** a team of 9 developers.
>
> My **main responsibilities include** assigning tasks, reviewing code, and helping the team overcome technical problems. I also **work on** core modules myself.
>
> One recent achievement — I **optimized** our permission management system by creating an automated script. It made our workflow much **smoother** across different environments.

## 五、常见错误 & 纠正

| 错误表达 | 问题 | 正确表达 |
|----------|------|----------|
| I have work 10 years. | 时态错误 | I have **worked** for 10 years. |
| I responsible for... | 缺少 be 动词 | I **am** responsible for... |
| I good at React. | 缺少 be 动词 | I **am** good at React. |
| I lead 9 persons. | persons 不自然 | I lead a team of 9 **developers**. |
| I do frontend since 2014. | 时态错误 | I **have been doing** frontend since 2014. |
| My experience is very much. | 用法不对 | I have **a lot of** experience. |

## 七、表达升级

从"能说"到"说得好"——同一个意思的不同表达层次：

| 基础说法 | 进阶说法 | 场景 |
|----------|----------|------|
| I do frontend work. | I specialize in frontend development. | 更专业 |
| I fix problems. | I troubleshoot and resolve technical issues. | 更精准 |
| I tell others what to do. | I assign tasks and coordinate with the team. | 更得体 |
| I made a tool. | I developed an automated tool that streamlined our workflow. | 更有影响力 |
| I want a new job. | I'm looking for new opportunities to grow. | 更积极 |

## 八、今日口语任务

### 任务 1：关键词串联（5 分钟）
只看下面的关键词，用英语串成一段自我介绍：
- **frontend / 10 years / team lead / 9 developers / React / Vue / Node.js scripts / new challenge**

### 任务 2：限时回答（5 分钟）
设定 2 分钟计时器，不看任何材料，回答 "Tell me about yourself"。录音。

### 任务 3：录音回听（5 分钟）
听自己的录音，找出：
- 哪里停顿超过 3 秒？
- 哪个词发音不确定？
- 有没有用到上面学的句式？

## 九、AI 对话练习

把以下 Prompt 复制到 ChatGPT 或 Claude 中进行练习：

```
You are a friendly interviewer at a foreign company in Chengdu.
We are doing a practice interview. I am a frontend developer with 10+ years of experience.

Rules for this practice:
1. Start by saying "Hi, thanks for coming in. Could you tell me a little about yourself?"
2. After I answer, give me feedback:
   - Rate my answer (1-5): clarity, fluency, content
   - Point out 1-2 grammar or vocabulary issues
   - Rewrite ONE of my sentences in a more natural way
   - Explain WHY the rewritten version is better
3. Then ask a follow-up: "Can you tell me more about your current role?"
4. After I answer that, give similar feedback
5. Finally, give me a model answer for "Tell me about yourself" based on what I shared

Keep your language simple and encouraging.
Use Chinese to explain grammar points if needed.
```
