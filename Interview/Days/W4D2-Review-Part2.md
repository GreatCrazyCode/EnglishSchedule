## Quick Review: Week 3 Questions

### Q: How much do you know about our company? (W3D1)
**Key points to hit:**
- Mention specific facts (products, tech stack, culture)
- Show you did research
- Keep it focused - 3-4 facts max

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I've done some research and I know that..."
- "I was impressed by..."
- "I understand that your company values..."

---

### Q: What attracted you to our company? (W3D1)
**Key points to hit:**
- Something specific (not "your company is great")
- Connect to your interests and goals
- Show genuine enthusiasm

**Must-use phrases:**
- "What attracted me most was..."
- "I'm particularly drawn to..."
- "This resonates with me because..."

---

### Q: Where do you see yourself in 5 years? (W3D2)
**Key points to hit:**
- Short-term: master the role, contribute
- Long-term: leadership, complex projects
- Connect goals to the company

**Must-use phrases:**
- "In the near term, I want to..."
- "Over time, I hope to..."
- "I think this role is a great stepping stone."

---

### Q: What are you looking for in your next role? (W3D2)
**Key points to hit:**
- Challenging projects
- Growth opportunities
- Good team culture

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I'm looking for..."
- "What matters most to me is..."

---

### Q: Why did you leave your last job? (W3D3)
**Key points to hit:**
- Be honest (layoff/restructuring)
- Stay positive, don't blame
- Pivot to future quickly

**Must-use phrases:**
- "The company went through restructuring..."
- "While it was disappointing, I see it as..."
- "I'm excited about what's ahead."

---

### Q: Salary expectations? (W3D4)
**Key points to hit:**
- Give a range
- Show flexibility
- Mention full compensation

**Must-use phrases:**
- "Based on my experience, I'm looking for a range of..."
- "I'm open to discussion."
- "I'd consider the overall compensation package."

---

### Q: Do you have questions for us? (W3D4)
**Key points to hit:**
- Have 2-3 prepared questions
- Ask about team, projects, culture
- Show genuine interest

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I'd love to know more about..."
- "I'm curious about..."

---

### Q: How do you handle criticism? (W3D4)
**Key points to hit:**
- Welcome it / Open to it
- Listen, think, then improve
- Don't take it personally

**Must-use phrases:**
- "I welcome constructive criticism."
- "I listen carefully and try to learn from it."
- "I see it as a chance to grow."

## Bonus Questions Quick Review

These questions haven't been covered in dedicated lessons, but the skills you've learned apply:

### Q: What are your hobbies? / What do you do in free time? (Q26, Q27)
> In my free time, I enjoy **coding personal projects** to practice new technologies. I also like **reading tech blogs** and watching online tutorials to stay updated. For fun, I enjoy watching movies and **spending time** with friends and family.

**Tip:** Keep it light but mention something tech-related to show passion.

---

### Q: Why Chengdu? (Q19)
> My **family** is here in Chengdu. It's where I've been **living and working** for over 10 years. It's **home** for me.

**Tip:** This is a simple question. Keep the answer short and sincere.
