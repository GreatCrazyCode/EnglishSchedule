## Useful Expressions

### Talking About Future Plans
- **In five years, I'd like to...**
  - In five years, I'd like to be leading complex projects.
- **I see myself [doing what] in [timeframe].**
  - I see myself growing into a senior technical role in the next few years.
- **My goal is to...**
  - My goal is to become a stronger leader and mentor.
- **I hope to...**
  - I hope to work on more challenging and impactful projects.
- **Eventually, I want to...**
  - Eventually, I want to have a bigger impact on the products I build.

### Describing Career Stages
- **In the short term, I plan to...**
  - In the short term, I plan to master your tech stack and contribute to the team.
- **Over time, I'd like to...**
  - Over time, I'd like to take on more leadership responsibilities.
- **In the long run, I envision...**
  - In the long run, I envision myself as a senior technical leader.

### Describing What You Want in a Role
- **I'm looking for [what].**
  - I'm looking for challenging projects that help me grow.
- **What matters most to me is [what].**
  - What matters most to me is working with a great team.
- **I value [what] in a workplace.**
  - I value continuous learning and open communication.

### Connecting Goals to the Company
- **I think this company can help me [goal].**
  - I think this company can help me achieve that goal.
- **That's one reason I'm excited about this role.**
- **Your company seems like the right place for this kind of growth.**

## Answer Framework

### Five-Year Plan: Short-term + Long-term + Connection

```
Short-term (1-2 years):
  "In the near future, I want to [immediate goals]."
  Focus: master the role, contribute to the team

Long-term (3-5 years):
  "Over time, I hope to [bigger goals]."
  Focus: leadership, larger projects, deeper expertise

Connection to company:
  "I think this role is a great step toward that goal."
```

### Next Role Expectations: 3 Key Things

```
1. Growth: "I want to keep learning and developing my skills."
2. Challenge: "I'm looking for meaningful, challenging work."
3. Team/Culture: "I want to work in a supportive, collaborative environment."
```

## Reference Answers

### Q: Where do you see yourself in five years?

> **In the near term**, I want to focus on becoming an even **stronger** frontend developer. I'd like to **master** the tech stack at your company, contribute to **key projects**, and build a good relationship with the team.
>
> **Over time**, I hope to take on more **complex projects** and more **leadership** responsibilities. I enjoy **mentoring** junior developers, and I'd like to do more of that.
>
> I'm also **interested in** AI and new technologies. I'd love to **explore** how these can be applied to frontend development in practical ways.
>
> I think this role is a great **stepping stone** toward those goals. Your company offers the kind of **challenging work** and **growth environment** I'm looking for.

Notes:
- "in the near term" 比 "first" 更专业
- "stepping stone" = 跳板/阶梯, 表示这个职位是成长路径的一部分
- 把目标和公司联系起来, 展示你不是要利用公司当跳板然后走人

### Q: What are the most important things you're looking for in your next role?

> In my next role, I'm looking for **three main things**.
>
> **First**, **challenging projects** that push me to grow. I don't want to do the same thing every day. I want to solve new problems and learn new things.
>
> **Second**, **opportunities to lead** and mentor others. I've enjoyed my experience as a team lead, and I want to continue developing in that direction.
>
> **Third**, a **positive team culture**. I do my best work in an environment where people communicate **openly** and **support** each other. I value a company that **invests in** its people.

Notes:
- "three main things" - 分层回答, 条理清晰
- "push me to grow" - 比 "help me grow" 更有力
- "invests in its people" = 投资于员工, 说明你看重公司对人才的重视

### Q: Could you project what you'd like to be doing five years from now?

> Five years from now, I'd like to be a **senior frontend developer** — or even a **tech lead** — working on **large, complex projects**.
>
> I want to be someone who can not only **build** things, but also **guide** and **mentor** a team to deliver **high-quality** software.
>
> I'm also curious about the **intersection** of AI and frontend development. In five years, I'd love to be working in that area — building **smarter**, more **intelligent** user experiences.

Notes:
- "intersection" = 交叉点/结合, 说两个领域的结合
- 回答简洁有力, 不超过1分钟

## Things NOT to Say

| Don't Say | Why | Say Instead |
|-----------|-----|-------------|
| I want to start my own company. | 显得你会离开 | I want to grow into a leadership role here. |
| I want your job. | 太冒犯了 | I hope to take on more responsibility over time. |
| I don't know. | 缺乏规划 | I'm excited to see where this role takes me. |
| I want to be a manager. | 可能对方团队没管理岗 | I'd like to grow in both technical depth and leadership. |

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Problem | Correct |
|-------|---------|---------|
| I hope to developed more projects. | 时态错误 | I hope to **develop** more projects. |
| In five years I will be a senior. | 太绝对 | In five years, I'd **like to be** a senior. |
| I want grow my skills. | 缺少 to | I want **to** grow my skills. |
| I interesting in AI. | 词性错误 | I'm **interested** in AI. |
