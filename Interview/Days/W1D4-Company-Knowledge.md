> 今日面试题：**Why do you want to work for our company?** / **Why do you choose our company?**
>
> 学习时间：60 分钟 | 核心目标：学会表达你对目标公司的了解和兴趣

## 二、实用表达句式

### 表达你了解公司
- **I've read/learned that your company [公司特点].**
  - I've learned that your company focuses on AI-powered solutions.
- **I noticed that you [公司的做法].**
  - I noticed that you use React and modern frontend tools.
- **From what I've seen, [你的观察].**
  - From what I've seen, your team values quality and innovation.

### 表达你被吸引
- **What attracted me was [什么吸引你].**
  - What attracted me was your focus on cutting-edge technology.
- **I'm particularly interested in [什么让你感兴趣].**
  - I'm particularly interested in the kind of projects you work on.
- **I'm impressed by [什么让你印象深刻].**
  - I'm impressed by your company's growth in the past few years.

### 表达匹配度
- **I think my skills are a good match for [什么].**
  - I think my skills are a good match for what you're looking for.
- **This aligns with my career goals because [原因].**
  - This aligns with my career goals because I want to work on complex frontend projects.
- **I believe I can contribute to [什么].**
  - I believe I can contribute to your team's success.

### 表达期待
- **I'm excited about the chance to [做什么].**
  - I'm excited about the chance to work with your talented team.
- **I'd love to [做什么].**
  - I'd love to be part of your team and grow together.

## 三、回答框架

### 三段式：了解 → 吸引 → 匹配

```
第一段：展示你做了功课
  → "I've looked into your company, and I know that [公司特点]."

第二段：说你被什么吸引
  → "What attracted me is [吸引你的点]. I think this is [为什么好]."

第三段：说你和公司的匹配
  → "I believe my [技能/经验] can contribute to [公司的目标]. 
     And I'm excited to [你的期待]."
```

## 四、参考回答（带详细注解）

### Q: Why do you want to work for our company?

> I've been **looking into** your company, and a few things really **stand out** to me.
>
> **First**, I noticed that you use a **modern tech stack** — React, TypeScript, and other tools that I'm very **familiar with**. That's exciting because I can **hit the ground running** and start contributing **right away**.
>
> **Second**, I'm **impressed by** your company culture. From what I've read, you value **open communication** and encourage people to **share ideas**. That's the kind of environment where I **do my best work**.
>
> **Also**, I'm always looking to **grow** and **take on** new challenges. I think this role would give me the chance to work on **interesting projects** and learn from a **talented team**.

**注解：**
- "looking into" = 研究/了解，比 "I searched your company" 更自然
- "hit the ground running" = 快速上手，面试常用习语
- "do my best work" = 发挥最好水平，比 "work well" 更有力
- 三个层次：技术匹配 → 文化认同 → 个人成长

### Q: Why do you choose our company?

> To be honest, there are a few reasons why your company **appeals to** me.
>
> I want to work on **meaningful projects** with a team that cares about **quality**. From what I've seen, your company does exactly that.
>
> Your tech stack is also very **suitable** for me — I have a lot of experience with the tools you use. I think I can **bring value** to the team **from day one**.
>
> And I'm excited about the **learning opportunities** here. I want to keep growing, and I feel like this is the **right place** for that.

**注解：**
- "appeals to me" = 吸引我，比 "attracts me" 更正式
- "from day one" = 从第一天起，表示你能快速贡献
- "the right place" = 合适的地方，简洁有力

## 六、常见错误 & 纠正

| 错误表达 | 问题 | 正确表达 |
|----------|------|----------|
| Your company is very good. | 太空洞 | Your company has a **great reputation for** innovation. |
| I want money. | 太直接 | I'm looking for a role with **growth opportunities**. |
| I choose your company because it's famous. | 不够具体 | I choose your company because **your projects align with my skills**. |
| I want to learn from your company. | 只谈索取 | I want to **contribute to** your team **and** keep growing. |
| Your company is cool. | 太随意 | Your company does **impressive** work in [领域]. |

## 八、表达升级

| 基础说法 | 进阶说法 | 效果 |
|----------|----------|------|
| I like your company. | I'm genuinely excited about what your company does. | 更有热情 |
| Your tech is good. | Your tech stack aligns well with my expertise. | 更专业 |
| I want to learn. | I'm eager to grow alongside a talented team. | 更有诚意 |
| Your company is big. | Your company has established itself as a leader in the field. | 更得体 |
| I can work here. | I'm confident I can hit the ground running and contribute from day one. | 更有冲击力 |

## 十、AI 对话练习

```
You are an interviewer at a foreign tech company in Chengdu. Your company:
- Builds web applications for international clients
- Uses React, TypeScript, and Node.js
- Values open communication and teamwork
- Has about 200 employees

Practice session:
1. Ask "So, why do you want to work for our company?"
2. After I answer, check:
   - Did I mention specific things about the company? (Score: 1-5 for specificity)
   - Did I connect the company to my own goals?
   - Rewrite my answer to be more specific and natural
3. Then challenge me: "But many companies use React. What makes us different to you?"
4. After I answer, ask "What do you know about our company culture?"
5. Give final feedback:
   - Overall score (1-5)
   - Did I sound genuine or generic?
   - Give me 3 phrases to memorize for this type of question
   
Use Chinese to explain when I make mistakes.
```
