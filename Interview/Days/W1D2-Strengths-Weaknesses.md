# Week 1 · Day 2 — 优势与不足

> 今日面试题：**What is your greatest strength?** / **What is your greatest weakness?**
>
> 学习时间：60 分钟 | 核心目标：学会用具体例子描述自己的优缺点

---

| 单词/短语 | 音标 | 中文 | 例句 |

## 二、实用表达句式

### 描述优势
- **My greatest strength is [名词/动名词].**
  - My greatest strength is problem-solving.
- **I would say I'm [形容词].**
  - I would say I'm a fast learner and very adaptable.
- **I'm really good at [doing something].**
  - I'm really good at figuring out complex technical problems.
- **One of my strengths is that I can [做什么].**
  - One of my strengths is that I can quickly adapt to new tech stacks.

### 给出具体例子
- **For example, [具体例子].**
  - For example, at my current company, I built a 3D rendering engine from scratch using Three.js.
- **Let me give you an example.**
  - Let me give you an example. Last year, I...
- **A good example would be when I [做了什么].**
  - A good example would be when I developed an automation tool for our team.

### 描述弱点
- **I would say my weakness is [弱点].**
  - I would say my weakness is public speaking.
- **Sometimes I tend to [不好的习惯].**
  - Sometimes I tend to focus too much on details.
- **I'm not always great at [某事].**
  - I'm not always great at presenting to large groups.

### 说你在改进
- **I'm working on [改进什么].**
  - I'm working on my presentation skills.
- **I've been trying to [具体行动].**
  - I've been trying to speak up more in large meetings.
- **To improve this, I [具体做法].**
  - To improve this, I've been practicing presenting to my team regularly.

## 三、回答框架

### Strength 框架：观点 → 例子 → 结果

```
第一句：说出你的优势
  → "I would say my greatest strength is [优势]."

第二句：给一个具体例子
  → "For example, at my current company, I [做了什么]."

第三句：说出结果/影响
  → "This [带来了什么结果]."
```

### Weakness 框架：承认 → 影响 → 改进

```
第一句：坦诚承认弱点
  → "I would say my weakness is [弱点]."

第二句：简要说影响（不要说太多）
  → "Sometimes this means I [什么影响]."

第三句：重点说改进行动
  → "To address this, I've been [做什么来改进]."
```

## 四、参考回答（带详细注解）

### Q: What is your greatest strength?

> I would say my greatest strength is **problem-solving**. I really enjoy **taking on** challenges and finding solutions.
>
> **For example**, at my current company, I developed a 3D rendering engine using Three.js. It was planned to **replace** an expensive business solution. The project was complex, but I **figured out** how to make it work step by step.
>
> I'm also a **fast learner**. I can **pick up** new technologies quickly and **adapt** to different project requirements. This has **helped** me a lot throughout my career.

**注解：**
- "taking on challenges" = 接受挑战，比 "I like challenges" 更自然
- "figured out" = 弄明白/搞定，非常口语化
- "pick up" = 学会（口语中比 learn 更自然）
- 注意整个回答的结构：优势 → 例子 → 补充强化

### Q: What is your greatest weakness?

> Honestly, I sometimes **get too focused on** the technical details. I want everything to be **perfect**, and I can **lose sight of** the bigger picture or the deadline.
>
> **I'm aware of** this, and I've been **working on** finding a better **balance** between quality and speed. Now I try to **set clear priorities** at the start of each task, so I know when something is "**good enough**" to move forward.

**注解：**
- "get too focused on" = 过于专注于，自然的表达
- "lose sight of" = 忽视了，习语表达
- "I'm aware of this" = 我意识到了，展示自我认知
- "good enough" = 足够好了，表示你在学习取舍
- 整个回答的感觉：诚实 + 有改进方案 = 成熟

### 避免这些弱点回答：

| 不要说 | 原因 |
|--------|------|
| "I'm too hard-working." | 太假了，面试官听腻了 |
| "I have no weakness." | 缺乏自我认知 |
| "I'm bad at teamwork." | 致命弱点，别提 |
| "I'm always late." | 职业素养问题 |

### 安全的弱点选择：
- Public speaking / presenting to large groups（公开演讲）
- Being too detail-oriented（过于注重细节）
- Having difficulty saying no（不太会拒绝人）
- Wanting to do everything yourself（不太善于委派任务）

## 五、常见错误 & 纠正

| 错误表达 | 问题 | 正确表达 |
|----------|------|----------|
| My strength is I work hard. | 结构不对 | My strength is **that** I work hard. |
| I will work hardly to improve. | hardly ≠ 努力 | I will work **hard** to improve. |
| I am weakness is... | 搞混了名词和形容词 | My **weakness** is... |
| I have not weakness. | 语法错误 | I **don't have any major** weaknesses. |
| I good at solve problems. | 缺 be 动词 + 动名词 | I'**m** good at **solving** problems. |

**特别注意：hard vs. hardly**
- hard = 努力地（I work hard.）
- hardly = 几乎不（I can hardly hear you.）
- 这两个词意思完全相反！

## 七、表达升级

| 基础说法 | 进阶说法 | 效果 |
|----------|----------|------|
| I can solve problems. | I have strong problem-solving skills. | 更专业 |
| I learn fast. | I'm a quick learner who adapts easily. | 更具体 |
| I'm not good at speaking. | I sometimes feel nervous presenting to large groups. | 更委婉得体 |
| I'm trying to be better. | I've been actively working on improving this. | 更有行动力 |
| I focus too much. | I tend to get caught up in the details. | 更地道 |

## 八、今日口语任务

### 任务 1：造句练习（5 分钟）
用以下词汇各造一个关于自己的句子（不要抄上面的）：
- strength / problem-solving / adapt / weakness / improve

### 任务 2：限时回答（10 分钟）
分别回答 strength 和 weakness，每个限时 1 分钟，录音。
- 检查：有没有用"观点 → 例子 → 结果"的结构？
- 检查：weakness 的回答有没有提到改进行动？

### 任务 3：对镜练习（5 分钟）
对着镜子（或开手机前置摄像头）说一遍，注意：
- 不要低头看材料
- 保持眼神接触
- 说错了不要停，继续说

## 九、AI 对话练习

```

- A score (1-5) for each answer
- The top 3 phrases I should memorize from today
- One thing I did well
```
