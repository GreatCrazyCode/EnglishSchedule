# Week 1 · Day 3 — 业绩展示

> 今日面试题：**Give me examples of your good performance** / **Why should we hire you?**
>
> 学习时间：60 分钟 | 核心目标：学会用英语展示工作成果，说服面试官录用你

## 二、实用表达句式

### 引出成就
- **One thing I'm proud of is [成就].**
  - One thing I'm proud of is the automation tool I built.
- **A good example is when I [做了什么].**
  - A good example is when I led the permission system redesign.
- **Let me share an achievement from my current role.**

### 描述你做了什么（Action）
- **I developed / built / created [什么].**
  - I developed a Node.js script to automate our build process.
- **I took the initiative to [做什么].**
  - I took the initiative to build a bug tracking website.
- **I identified the problem and [解决方案].**
  - I identified the problem and created a solution.

### 描述结果（Result）
- **This led to [结果].**
  - This led to faster deployments and fewer errors.
- **As a result, [结果].**
  - As a result, the company rewarded me 2000 yuan.
- **It improved [什么] by [多少].**
  - It improved our team's efficiency significantly.
- **This saved us [时间/金钱].**
  - This saved us hours of manual work every week.

### "Why hire me" 的句式
- **I believe I'm a strong fit because [原因].**
  - I believe I'm a strong fit because of my experience and adaptability.
- **I bring [什么] to the table.**
  - I bring both technical skills and leadership experience to the table.
- **What sets me apart is [区别].**
  - What sets me apart is my ability to solve problems creatively.

## 三、回答框架

### 展示业绩：用 STAR 简化版

```
S (Situation): 简单背景，一句话
  → "At my current company, [背景情况]."

A (Action): 你做了什么，2-3句话
  → "I [具体行动]. I also [补充行动]."

R (Result): 结果/数据，1-2句话
  → "As a result, [具体成效]."
```

### "Why hire me"：三要素法

```
技能匹配：我的技术栈和你们的需求一致
  → "I have strong skills in [技术], which matches your needs."

经验证明：我有类似的成功经验
  → "I've already done [类似的事] in my current role."

额外价值：我还能带来什么
  → "Plus, I'm [额外优势]."
```

## 四、参考回答（带详细注解）

### Q: Give me one or two examples of your good performance

> Sure. Let me share two examples.
>
> **First**, at my current company, I **led** a team of 9 frontend developers. I was **responsible for** task assignment, code reviews, and solving technical challenges. We **successfully delivered** several major projects **on time**.
>
> **Second**, I **noticed** that our permission management process was too **manual** and **error-prone**. So I **took the initiative** to develop a Node.js script that **automated** the whole process. Different environments could now **build** with different permission settings automatically. This **streamlined** our workflow and **reduced** errors. The team was much more **efficient** after that.

**注解：**
- "Let me share two examples" — 开头有条理
- "took the initiative" — 展示主动性，面试官很看重
- "error-prone" = 容易出错的，好用的形容词
- "streamlined" = 优化流程，面试高频词
- 用 First / Second 分层，逻辑清晰

### Q: Why should we hire you?

> I think I'd be a **great fit** for this role for a few reasons.
>
> **First**, I have over 10 years of frontend experience. I **mainly work with** React and Vue, which I believe **matches** your tech stack.
>
> **Second**, I'm not just a developer — I also have **leadership** experience. I've led a team of 9 people, so I understand how to **collaborate** and **deliver** results as a team.
>
> **Third**, I'm a **fast learner** and I always look for ways to **improve** things. For example, I built automation tools that saved our team a lot of time. I think I can **bring that same mindset** to your company.

**注解：**
- "great fit" = 非常合适，面试万能词
- 三个原因分层：技能 → 经验 → 态度
- "bring that same mindset" = 把同样的思维方式带到你们公司

## 五、常见错误 & 纠正

| 错误表达 | 问题 | 正确表达 |
|----------|------|----------|
| The company reward me 2000. | 时态、没有过去式 | The company **rewarded** me 2000 yuan. |
| This improve our efficiency. | 第三人称/时态 | This **improved** our efficiency. |
| I develop a website to show details. | 时态 | I **developed** a website to show details. |
| I can deliver high-quality software on time. ✓ | 这个是对的！ | — |
| I am wide range of skills. | 不能 am + 名词短语 | I **have** a wide range of skills. |
| permission can build different | 表达不清 | Permissions **can be configured differently** for each environment. |

## 七、表达升级

| 基础说法 | 进阶说法 | 效果 |
|----------|----------|------|
| I made a tool. | I developed an automation tool. | 更专业 |
| It was good for the team. | It significantly improved team efficiency. | 更量化 |
| I worked hard. | I took the initiative and went above and beyond. | 更有冲击力 |
| I can do this job. | I'm confident I can contribute meaningfully to your team. | 更得体 |
| I want to help. | I'm eager to bring my skills and experience to this role. | 更积极 |

## 八、今日口语任务

### 任务 1：列出你的成就清单（5 分钟）
用英语写出你最得意的 3 个工作成果，每个用一句话：
- I did X, which led to Y.

### 任务 2：STAR 练习（10 分钟）
选一个成就，用 S-A-R 框架完整讲一遍，录音。
检查：
- S 有没有超过 1 句话？（太长了要精简）
- A 有没有说清楚你个人做了什么？
- R 有没有具体结果？

### 任务 3：电梯演讲（5 分钟）
想象你只有 30 秒回答 "Why should we hire you?"，练习精简版回答。

## 九、AI 对话练习

```
You are a hiring manager at a foreign tech company in Chengdu. 
You are interviewing me for a Senior Frontend Developer position.

Practice session:
1. Ask "Can you share one or two examples of your best work performance?"
2. After I answer, give feedback:
   - Did I use specific results or numbers?
   - Was my story structured clearly? (Situation → Action → Result)
   - Rewrite my answer to sound more impactful
   - Explain the changes in Chinese
3. Then ask "That's great. So, why should we hire you over other candidates?"
4. After I answer, check:
   - Did I connect my skills to the job?
   - Did I sound confident?
   - Suggest stronger phrases I could use
5. Give me a final score (1-5) and 3 key takeaways

Keep language simple and conversational.
```
