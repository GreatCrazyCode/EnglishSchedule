## Key Rules for "Why Did You Leave?"

### The Golden Rules:
1. **NEVER badmouth** your previous company or boss
2. **Stay positive** - focus on the future, not the past
3. **Be honest** but brief - don't over-explain
4. **Pivot quickly** to what you're looking for now

### Safe Reasons to Give:
- Company layoffs/restructuring (it's OK to be honest about this)
- Looking for new challenges
- Career growth / want to learn more
- Relocation
- Company closed / project ended

### Dangerous Reasons to Avoid:
- "My boss was terrible"
- "I hated the work"
- "The salary was too low" (save this for salary negotiation)
- "My coworkers were annoying"

## Useful Expressions

### Explaining Layoffs (Positive Framing)
- **The company went through a round of layoffs.**
  - Unfortunately, the company went through a round of layoffs.
- **They had to let some employees go, including me.**
- **While it was disappointing, I see it as a chance to...**
  - While it was disappointing, I see it as a chance to find an even better fit.
- **It gave me the opportunity to rethink what I really want.**

### Pivoting to Positive
- **I'm now looking for [what you want].**
  - I'm now looking for a role where I can grow and take on new challenges.
- **This experience made me realize [what].**
  - This experience made me realize what I truly value in a job.
- **I see this as a fresh start.**
- **I'm excited about what's ahead.**

### Talking About Location
- **My family is here in [city].**
  - My family is here in Chengdu, so this is home for me.
- **I've been working here for [years].**
  - I've been working in Chengdu for over 10 years.
- **I'm very familiar with the city.**
- **It's where I've built my career.**

## Answer Framework

### Why Did You Leave: Fact + Feeling + Future

```
Fact (1 sentence - what happened):
  "The company went through layoffs / I was looking for new challenges."

Feeling (1 sentence - be positive):
  "While it was disappointing, I understand it was a business decision."

Future (1-2 sentences - what you want now):
  "I see it as a chance to find a role that's an even better fit."
  "I'm excited to bring my skills to a new team."
```

### Why This City: Simple and Sincere

```
Personal connection (1 sentence):
  "My family lives here, so Chengdu is home."

Professional connection (1 sentence):
  "I've built my career here over the past 10+ years."

Short and done - don't overthink this question.
```

## Reference Answers

### Q: Why did you leave your last job?

> **Unfortunately**, my previous company went through a **restructuring** and had to **downsize**. They **let go of** several employees, including me.
>
> **While** it was **disappointing** to leave, I **understand** it was a business decision. These things happen, and I don't hold any **hard feelings**.
>
> Actually, it's given me the chance to **step back** and think about what I really want in my next role. I'm now looking for a position where I can **make a bigger impact** and continue to **grow** as a developer.
>
> I'm **excited** about this opportunity because I think it's a **great fit** for where I want to go in my career.

Notes:
- "unfortunately" - 一个词表达遗憾, 不需要长篇抱怨
- "let go of" - 比 "fired" 更委婉
- "hard feelings" = 怨恨, 表示你没有负面情绪
- "step back" = 退一步思考, 展示成熟
- 迅速转向积极: "I'm excited about this opportunity"

### Q: Why do you want to work in Chengdu?

> It's simple, really. My **family** is here in Chengdu. This is **where I've been living and working** for over 10 years.
>
> I **know the city well**, I have a strong **professional network** here, and it's **home** for me. I'm very **comfortable** here and I want to continue building my career in this city.

Notes:
- 这个问题不需要长回答
- "It's simple, really" - 自然的开场
- "professional network" = 职业人脉
- 简洁 = 自信

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Problem | Correct |
|-------|---------|---------|
| The company cuts down the persons. | 语法混乱 | The company **laid off** several employees. |
| They fire me. | 太直接+时态 | They **let me go** as part of restructuring. |
| My boss was bad. | 说坏话 | I was looking for a **new direction**. |
| I leave because salary low. | 语法错误 | I left because I was seeking **better opportunities**. |
| I want work Chengdu because family. | 缺少介词和动词 | I want to work **in** Chengdu because my family **is** here. |

## Practice Tasks

### Task 1: Positive Pivot (10 min)
Practice saying this sequence 3 times, smoothly:
1. "Unfortunately, the company restructured."
2. "They had to let some people go, including me."
3. "While it was disappointing, I see it as a new opportunity."
4. "I'm excited to find a role that's a great fit for my skills."

Record yourself. Check: Does it flow naturally? Do you sound calm and positive?

### Task 2: 30-Second City Answer (5 min)
Answer "Why Chengdu?" in under 30 seconds. Keep it simple.

### Task 3: Follow-Up Prep (5 min)
Practice answering these follow-ups:
- "How did that experience shape your job search?"
- "What did you learn from that situation?"
