| 阶段 | 时间 | 内容 |

| 理解 | 15 min | 阅读当日问题 & 参考答案，标出关键词，理解回答逻辑 |

## Week 1：自我介绍 & 核心素质

### Day 1 — 自我介绍专题

**今日问题：**
- Q3: Can you tell me about yourself?
- Q2: Tell me about yourself / your current job

**回答要点：**
- 控制在 2 分钟以内，结构：当前角色 → 核心经验 → 为什么对这个机会感兴趣
- 不要流水账讲简历，挑 2-3 个亮点
- 用简单句，少用从句，说不出来就换个简单的说法

**内化方法：**
1. 写 3 个关于自己工作的关键词（比如 frontend / 10 years / team lead）
2. 只看关键词，尝试用英语串成一段话
3. 录音听自己说的，找到停顿多的地方重点练

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are a friendly interviewer at a foreign company in Chengdu. 
Start the interview by saying "Hi, thanks for coming in today. Could you tell me a little about yourself?"
After I answer, give me brief feedback:
1. Was my answer clear and natural?
2. Point out 1-2 grammar issues (if any)
3. Suggest a more natural way to say one of my sentences
Then ask a follow-up question about my current job.
Keep your language simple and conversational.
```

---

### Day 2 — 优势与不足

**今日问题：**
- Q5: What is your greatest strength?
- Q6: What is your greatest weakness?

**回答要点：**
- Strength：选一个和岗位相关的，用一个具体例子支撑（不要只说 "I'm hard-working"）
- Weakness：选一个真实但不致命的弱点，重点说你在怎么改进
- 公式：观点 + 例子 + 结果

**内化方法：**
1. 想一个你真的擅长的事，回忆一个具体场景
2. 用"因为...所以...结果..."的逻辑链条，先用中文理清，再用英语说
3. Weakness 的套路：承认 → 说影响 → 说改进行动

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are interviewing me for a frontend developer position.
Ask me "What would you say is your greatest strength?" 
After I answer, ask a follow-up like "Can you give me a specific example?"
Then ask about my weakness.
Give feedback on:
1. Did I sound confident or hesitant?
2. Was my example specific enough?
3. Suggest a smoother way to express one of my ideas
```

---

### Day 3 — 业绩展示

**今日问题：**
- Q1: Give me one or two examples to show your good performance
- Q12: Why should we hire you?

**回答要点：**
- 用 STAR 简化版：情况 → 我做了什么 → 结果是什么
- 数字和结果最有说服力（"improved efficiency", "saved time", "got rewarded"）
- "Why hire you" 要把自己的优势和岗位需求对接

**内化方法：**
1. 列出 3 个你最得意的工作成果
2. 每个成果用一句英语概括：I did X, which led to Y
3. 练习把这些成果串进 "Why hire me" 的回答里

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are a hiring manager. Ask me "Can you share an example of something you're proud of at work?"
After I answer, probe deeper: "What was the result?" or "How did that help the team?"
Then ask "So, why should we hire you over other candidates?"
Feedback focus:
1. Did I use specific results/numbers?
2. Did I connect my strengths to the job?
3. Help me rephrase one sentence to sound more impactful
```

---

### Day 4 — 公司认知

**今日问题：**
- Q4: Why do you want to work for our company?
- Q22: Why do you choose our company?

**回答要点：**
- 提前了解目标公司（业务、产品、技术栈、文化）
- 公式：我了解到你们公司... + 这和我的目标/技能很匹配 + 我很期待...
- 不要只说 "Your company is great"，要具体

**内化方法：**
1. 准备一个通用模板，留出可替换的 "公司名/产品/技术" 的空位
2. 选一个你感兴趣的公司，填入信息，练习说出来
3. 练到不看笔记也能自然表达

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are an interviewer at [公司名]. Your company focuses on [业务方向], 
uses React/Vue tech stack, and values open communication.
Ask me "Why do you want to join us?"
After I answer, challenge me: "What specifically about our company appeals to you?"
Feedback:
1. Did my answer feel genuine or generic?
2. Did I show I researched the company?
3. Suggest how to make my answer more specific
```

## Week 2：工作经验 & 应对挑战

### Day 1 — 工作经历概述

**今日问题：**
- Q18: Could you please tell us something more about your working experience?
- Q13: Can you describe a challenging project you worked on?

**回答要点：**
- 工作经历：宏观概括（年限 + 技术 + 角色），不要逐个公司讲
- 项目挑战：聚焦一个项目，讲清楚难在哪、你怎么解决的、结果如何
- 技术细节不要太深，面试官可能不是技术人员

**内化方法：**
1. 用 3 句话概括你的工作经历（年限、技术、角色变化）
2. 挑一个最有挑战的项目，画一个简单流程：问题 → 行动 → 结果
3. 对着流程图，用英语讲这个故事

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are interviewing me. Start by asking "Can you walk me through your work experience?"
After I give an overview, pick one thing I mentioned and ask "Tell me more about that project."
Then ask "What was the biggest challenge in that project?"
Feedback:
1. Was my overview too long or too short?
2. Did I tell a clear story about the project?
3. Point out where I can be more concise
```

---

### Day 2 — 处理困难与压力

**今日问题：**
- Q8: Can you describe a challenging situation you've faced at work and how you handled it?
- Q9: How do you handle stress and pressure?

**回答要点：**
- 困难情况用 STAR：Situation → Task → Action → Result
- 压力管理：展示你有方法论（计划、优先级、沟通）
- 语气要积极，困难是成长的机会

**内化方法：**
1. 回忆一次真实的工作困难，用中文列出 STAR 四个要素
2. 把每个要素翻译成一句简单英语
3. 连起来讲，注意用 "First... Then... Finally..." 等连接词

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are a senior manager interviewing me.
Ask "Tell me about a time you faced a really tough situation at work."
After I answer, ask "How did you stay calm during that?"
Then ask "How do you generally manage stress?"
Feedback:
1. Did I use a clear structure (situation → action → result)?
2. Did I sound calm and professional?
3. Suggest better transition phrases between my points
```

---

### Day 3 — 技术学习 & 岗位匹配

**今日问题：**
- Q14: What latest technology have you been learning recently?
- Q28: Why are you qualified for this position?

**回答要点：**
- 技术学习：展示学习热情，但要和岗位有关联
- 岗位匹配：经验 + 技能 + 学习能力，三位一体
- 少用 "I think"，多用 "I have / I can / I've done"

**内化方法：**
1. 列出你最近学的 2-3 个技术，每个用一句话说清楚是什么、为什么学
2. 把这些和目标岗位连接起来：学这个 → 能帮公司做什么
3. 练习用 "I'm qualified because..." 开头，串联你的优势

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
Ask me "What new technologies have you been exploring lately?"
After I answer, ask "How do you think that could help in this role?"
Then ask "What makes you think you're the right fit for this position?"
Feedback:
1. Did I connect my learning to the job?
2. Did I sound enthusiastic but not over-the-top?
3. Help me simplify any overly complex sentences
```

### Day 4 — 团队合作 & 冲突处理

**今日问题：**
- Q15: Describe a time when you had to work with a difficult team member
- Q21: How do you handle conflicts with your colleagues?

**回答要点：**
- 永远不要说别人坏话，重点在你怎么解决问题
- 展示沟通能力：倾听 → 理解 → 寻求共识
- 结尾要积极：冲突解决后关系变好了 / 效率提升了

**内化方法：**
1. 想一个真实的团队摩擦经历，不需要很严重的
2. 梳理：发生了什么 → 你怎么处理的 → 结果如何
3. 重点练习这些表达：
   - "I tried to understand their perspective"
   - "We found a solution that worked for both of us"
   - "After that, our teamwork improved"

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are an HR interviewer. Ask me "Have you ever had a disagreement with a coworker? What happened?"
After I answer, ask "What did you learn from that experience?"
Then ask "In general, how do you handle workplace conflicts?"
Feedback:
1. Did I avoid blaming the other person?
2. Did I show good communication skills?
3. Suggest more diplomatic phrases I could use
```

## Week 3：职业规划 & 实用场景

### Day 1 — 公司了解 & 吸引力

**今日问题：**
- Q23: How much do you know about our company?
- Q24: Why do you want to work for this company?
- Q25: What attracted you to our company?

**回答要点：**
- 这三题本质相同，准备一套素材可以灵活应对
- 需要提前做功课：公司官网、产品、新闻、技术博客
- 结构：我了解到... → 我被...吸引 → 因为这和我的...一致

**内化方法：**
1. 选一个目标公司，花 10 分钟浏览官网
2. 记下 3 个关键点（产品/技术/文化）
3. 用这 3 个点组装回答，练习自然切换

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
Pretend you are from [目标公司名]. We make [产品/服务].
Ask me these three questions one by one:
1. "What do you know about us?"
2. "Why do you want to work here?"
3. "What attracted you to apply?"
After each answer, rate my specificity 1-5 and suggest how to be more concrete.
Keep the conversation natural and encouraging.
```

---

### Day 2 — 职业规划

**今日问题：**
- Q7: Where do you see yourself in five years?
- Q17: Could you project what you would like to be doing five years from now?
- Q20: What are the most important things you are looking for in your next role?

**回答要点：**
- 五年规划要务实，和公司发展方向一致
- 不要说 "I want to start my own company"
- Next role 的期望要积极：成长 + 挑战 + 好的团队

**内化方法：**
1. 想清楚你真的想要什么（技术深度？管理？AI方向？）
2. 把你的规划分成 2 个阶段讲：短期（1-2年）和中期（3-5年）
3. 练习用 "I'd like to..." 和 "I hope to..." 表达期望

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
Ask me "Where do you see yourself in five years?"
After I answer, ask "That's interesting. What would be the first step to get there?"
Then ask "What's most important to you in your next job?"
Feedback:
1. Did my plan sound realistic?
2. Did I align my goals with the company's growth?
3. Suggest more natural ways to talk about future plans
```

### Day 3 — 离职原因 & 地点选择

**今日问题：**
- Q16: Why did you leave your last job?
- Q19: Why do you want to work at Chengdu?

**回答要点：**
- 离职原因：保持正面，不说前公司坏话
- 裁员可以坦诚说，但要转向积极面：new opportunity
- 地点选择：家庭 + 熟悉 + 城市发展

**内化方法：**
1. 离职原因准备一个 30 秒版本，简洁有力
2. 练习自然过渡："While it was disappointing... I see it as..."
3. 地点问题简单回答即可，不需要展开太多

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are an HR manager. Ask me "Why did you leave your previous company?"
If my answer mentions layoffs, follow up sensitively: "I understand. How did that experience shape your job search?"
Then ask "Why Chengdu specifically?"
Feedback:
1. Did I stay positive about my former employer?
2. Did I pivot to future opportunities naturally?
3. Help me sound more confident in my answer
```

### Day 4 — 实用问题

**今日问题：**
- Q10: What are your salary expectations?
- Q11: Do you have any questions for us?
- Q29: How do you normally handle criticism?

**回答要点：**
- 薪资：给范围不给具体数字，表示愿意协商
- 反问面试官：准备 2-3 个有质量的问题（团队、项目、发展）
- 批评处理：展示成熟的心态，听 → 思考 → 改进

**内化方法：**
1. 确定自己的薪资范围，练习自然地说出来（不要紧张）
2. 准备 3 个万能反问问题，烂熟于心
3. 批评处理：想一个真实被批评的经历，练习正面描述

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
We're nearing the end of the interview.
Ask me "What are your salary expectations?"
After I answer, ask "Do you have any questions for us?"
Then ask "One more thing - how do you handle criticism from your manager?"
Feedback:
1. Was I comfortable discussing salary?
2. Were my questions for the company thoughtful?
3. Did I show maturity in handling criticism?
```

## Week 4：综合复习 & 模拟面试

### Day 1 — 复习 Week 1 + 2 核心题

**复习内容：** Q1-Q9, Q12, Q13

**练习方式：**
1. 快速回顾每题的关键词（不看参考答案）
2. 每题用 1 分钟回答，录音
3. 听录音找问题：卡顿、语法、逻辑

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are conducting a rapid-fire interview round. 
Ask me these questions one by one, give me about 1 minute for each:
1. Tell me about yourself
2. What's your greatest strength?
3. Give me an example of good work performance
4. Why our company?
5. Describe a challenging project
After all 5 answers, give me an overall score (1-10) and top 3 things to improve.
```

---

### Day 2 — 复习 Week 3 核心题

**复习内容：** Q14-Q29

**练习方式：**
1. 随机抽 5 题，不看材料直接回答
2. 对比参考答案，看遗漏了什么关键点
3. 重点补强薄弱题目

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
Randomly pick 5 questions from these topics and ask me:
- Why should we hire you?
- How do you handle conflicts?
- What are your salary expectations?
- What new tech are you learning?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
- Why did you leave your last job?
- Do you have questions for us?
Don't tell me which ones in advance. After all answers, rank them from best to worst.
```

---

### Day 3 — 流利度专项训练

**今日重点：** 不纠结对错，只关注说得流畅

**练习方式：**
1. 设定计时器，每题限时 90 秒
2. 说不出来不要停，用简单词替代
3. 练习常用过渡句：
   - "That's a great question..."
   - "Let me think about that for a moment..."
   - "To give you an example..."
   - "What I mean is..."

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
We're going to practice fluency. Rules:
1. Ask me random interview questions
2. I must answer within 10 seconds of each question
3. If I pause too long, prompt me with "Take your time, but keep going"
4. After 5 questions, tell me:
   - How fluent did I sound? (1-10)
   - Did I use filler words too much?
   - What phrases could I use to buy thinking time naturally?
Focus on my flow, not grammar perfection.
```

### Day 4 — 全真模拟面试

**今日重点：** 完整模拟一场 30 分钟面试

**AI 对话练习 Prompt：**
```
You are an interviewer at a foreign tech company in Chengdu, interviewing me for a Senior Frontend Developer position. 

Conduct a complete 30-minute interview:
1. Start with small talk (weather, how I got here, etc.)
2. Ask about my background (2-3 questions)
3. Ask about technical experience (2-3 questions)  
4. Ask behavioral questions (2-3 questions)
5. Ask about career goals and salary
6. Let me ask questions

Rules:
- Use simple, conversational English
- React naturally to my answers (nod, say "I see", ask follow-ups)
- If I struggle, gently help me along
- At the end, give me a detailed evaluation:
  * Overall impression (1-10)
  * Fluency (1-10)
  * Content quality (1-10)
  * Confidence level (1-10)
  * Top 3 strengths
  * Top 3 areas to improve
  * Specific sentences I should practice more
```

## 日常小贴士

1. **不要追求完美** — 外企面试英语不需要像 native speaker，流利 + 自信 > 语法完美
2. **用简单句** — "I did X. It helped Y." 比复杂从句更清楚
3. **准备万能句式** — 遇到不会的题可以用：
   - "That's a good question. In my experience..."
   - "I would say that..."
   - "For example, in my current role..."
4. **每天 5 分钟英语自言自语** — 描述你在做什么，激活英语思维
5. **录音回听** — 这是最快发现问题的方法
6. **别怕犯错** — 面试官更看重你能不能沟通，而不是语法完不完美
