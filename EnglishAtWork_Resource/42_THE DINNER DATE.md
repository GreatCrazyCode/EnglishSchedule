## Introduction

Anna and Tom are at a pizza restaurant for their dinner date, but Anna has work on her mind. She needs to put together a proposal for her new business idea!

## Key Phrases

- Exceptional!
- Fantastic!
- Quality.
- Profit.
- Efficiency.
- Value for money.

## Listening Challenge

What is Anna's 'brilliant' new idea?
