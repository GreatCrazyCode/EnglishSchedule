**AN INTRODUCTION**

**EPISODE 160701 / 01 JUL 2016**

Meet Anna, Paul, Denise and Tom.

English at Work focuses on English communication in the office. Join Neil in this special introductory programme as he finds out about the characters who work in the offices of Tip Top Trading.

Key phrases in this episode:
*   A good example that comes to mind...
*   I'm particularly proud of...
*   Timekeeping is important to me.

**TRANSCRIPT**

**Narrator**: Hello, I'm Neil. Thanks for joining me for English at Work - a new series of programmes set in an office and full of useful tips to help you learn some business English you can use in the workplace. In the next few minutes you can join me for a tour of one of London's biggest manufacturers of imitation plastic fruit, called Tip Top Trading. We're going to hear from some of the staff who work hard to make sure the business runs smoothly. So, come on! Let's step inside the office and eavesdrop on a possible new member of staff at Tip Top Trading. I say 'possible' because she's still having her interview for the job of Sales Executive...

**Anna**: Firstly, the job matches my skills and experience perfectly: I've been working in sales for many years and I get on with people easily. Well, I mean, apart from the ones I don't like! And secondly, I know Tip Top Trading is one of the fastest growing companies in London and I want to be part of that.

**Paul**: Exactly. Tip Top Trading is the fastest-growing company in the plastic fruit sector.

**Narrator**: Well said Anna! If she gets the job I'm sure she'll be a great asset to the company. The decision lies with the manager, Paul, who we heard just then. He's a really nice chap, a bit disorganised, but when things go wrong, he has to take charge... like this...

**Paul**: It wasn't a good day yesterday. Two clients came in with serious complaints. Mrs Kumquat received a delivery of purple bananas - not very realistic - and Mr Lime, who ordered grapefruits, received pineapples instead. Tom!

**Tom**: Uh, look...

**Paul**: Were you responsible for these mistakes?

**Tom**: Er... yes I was, but--

**Paul**: Look, mistakes happen. But Mrs Kumquat left our office seeming angrier than when she arrived and she said she'd never use Tip Top Trading again!

**Tom**: I did my best.

**Paul**: Hmmm.

**Narrator**: Ah yes, Tom! I haven't told you about Tom yet, he's one of the company's top Sales Executives - he's brilliant...

**Tom**: [On the phone] Tom speaking. Yes! Frankie! What's the latest, are we on?

**Narrator**: ...but his people skills need some work. Listen to this!

**Tom**: My computer has crashed. I've lost my phone. My schedule is in a real mess. I've got two meetings booked at the same time with two extremely important clients. I can't be in two places at once!

**Narrator**: You get the picture! Now, every office needs a good office assistant - and Tip Top Trading is no exception. It has Denise, who assists, organises and sometimes makes the tea...

**Denise**: Oh, excuse me, sorry to interrupt, Paul, here's your tea.

**Paul**: Thanks Denise.

**Narrator**: But boy! Does she like to talk...

**Denise**: Really! "Denise do this! Denise do that!" I'm telling you Sharon, I've had enough! I'm being treated like a dogsbody!

**Narrator**: So that's Denise! I think we'll leave the office for now and let them get on with their work. So that's Tip Top Trading. There are plenty of other people we'll meet along the way - so, come on - why not join me and listen to English at Work from bbclearningenglish.com. Bye for now!

**LISTENING CHALLENGE - ANSWER**
What type of company is Tip Top Trading?
Tip Top Trading is a manufacturer of plastic fruit.
