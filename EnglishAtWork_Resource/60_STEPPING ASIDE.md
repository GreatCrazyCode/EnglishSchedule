## Introduction

The team from Tip Top Trading has been having a smashing time at the launch of its plastic aubergine. Tom threw one at Dave from the IT department, but it missed him and went through a window! It's time for Paul to hand out some harsh words but he's about to hear some words he won't like. What will Mr Socrates think of it all?

## Key Phrases

- I've been doing a strategic review of the company.
- I need to downsize.
- I need you to step aside.
- The company needs new direction and more dynamic leadership.
- You are surplus to requirement.
