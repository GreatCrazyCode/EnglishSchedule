## Introduction

At the offices of Tip Top Trading, things are a bit strange after Tom walked out in a huff – or a mood – after realising that Anna was never going to marry him. Can Anna get down to business and start managing the company or is there something on her mind that she needs to sort out?

## Key Phrases

- I'll be advertising for a replacement for Tom today.
- I want to make some changes in the office.
- I need to turn this business round.
- I'm keen to hear your ideas.

## Listening Challenge

What phrase can mean to admit you are wrong?
