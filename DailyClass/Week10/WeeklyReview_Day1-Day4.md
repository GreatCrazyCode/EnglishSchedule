
# Week 10 Review: Day 1 - Day 4

## Introduction (引言)
This week, we covered key aspects of professional development and business processes. We moved from reviewing personal performance and ensuring workplace safety to the exciting stages of developing and researching new business ideas.
本周，我们涵盖了专业发展和业务流程的关键方面。我们从个人绩效评估和确保工作场所安全，转向了开发和研究新商业想法的激动人心的阶段。

## Topic Review (主题回顾)
*   **Day 1: Performance Appraisals (绩效评估)**
    *   We learned how to discuss our achievements and areas for improvement, and how to set future "objectives" in a performance "appraisal".
    *   我们学习了如何在绩效“评估”中讨论我们的成就和需要改进的领域，以及如何设定未来的“目标”。

*   **Day 2: Health and Safety (健康与安全)**
    *   We focused on the correct "procedure" for reporting a workplace "accident" and the importance of clear documentation.
    *   我们专注于报告工作场所“事故”的正确“程序”以及清晰文档的重要性。

*   **Day 3: Writing a Business Proposal (撰写商业建议书)**
    *   We practiced using positive and persuasive language (like "exceptional" and "fantastic") to create a compelling business "proposal".
    *   我们练习了使用积极和有说服力的语言（如“卓越的”和“极好的”）来创建引人注目的商业“建议书”。

*   **Day 4: Conducting Market Research (进行市场研究)**
    *   We learned how to carry out market "research" for a new product by conducting a customer "survey" to gather opinions.
    *   我们学习了如何通过进行客户“调查”以收集意见，从而为新产品进行市场“研究”。

## Core Vocabulary and Expressions (核心词汇与表达式)

### Performance & Development (绩效与发展)
*   **Vocabulary:** appraisal, performance, objectives, review, compliment
*   **Expressions:** 
    *   What has gone well for you this year?
    *   What didn't go so well?
    *   Let's look ahead and set some objectives.

### Safety & Procedure (安全与程序)
*   **Vocabulary:** accident, injury, procedure, cause, avoid, first-aider
*   **Expressions:**
    *   How did the accident happen?
    *   What was the cause of the accident?
    *   How could the accident have been avoided?

### Innovation & Research (创新与研究)
*   **Vocabulary:** proposal, exceptional, fantastic, quality, profit, efficiency, value for money, research, survey, opinion, currently
*   **Expressions:**
    *   This exceptional product has fantastic potential.
    *   Our customers will get value for money.
    *   Could I ask for your opinion on a new product?

## Comprehensive Exercise (综合练习)
**Scenario:** You are in your yearly "appraisal". Your manager asks what your "objectives" are for next year. You want to present your new product idea.

**Task:** Write a short dialogue. First, tell your manager your objective is to develop a new product. Then, give a short "proposal" for your idea, explaining that you have already done some "research" and believe it will bring great "profit". Use at least **4** of the key words or expressions from this week.

---
**(情景练习):** 你正在进行年度“评估”。你的经理问你明年的“目标”是什么。你想要展示你的新产品想法。

**任务:** 写一段简短的对话。首先，告诉你的经理你的目标是开发一个新产品。然后，为你的想法做一个简短的“提议”，解释你已经做了一些“研究”，并相信它会带来巨大的“利润”。请至少使用本周学到的 **4个** 关键词或表达式。
