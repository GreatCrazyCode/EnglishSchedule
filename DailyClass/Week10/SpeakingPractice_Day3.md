
# Day 3: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Writing a Business Proposal (撰写商业建议书)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **proposal** (建议)
*   **exceptional** (卓越的)
*   **fantastic potential** (极好的潜力)
*   **value for money** (物有所值)
*   **profit** (利润)
*   **efficiency** (效率)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever had a "brilliant idea"? What was it?
    (你曾有过“绝妙的主意”吗？是什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What makes a business proposal persuasive?
    (什么能让一份商业建议书有说服力？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it important to talk about "profit" and "efficiency" in a business proposal?
    (为什么在商业建议书中谈论“利润”和“效率”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** What does "value for money" mean? Can you give an example of a product that you think offers good value for money?
    (“物有所值”是什么意思？你能举一个你认为物有所值的产品例子吗？)

5.  **Extension:** Think of a simple, everyday product. How could you describe it using "powerful" and "positive" language like "exceptional" and "fantastic"?
    (想一个简单的日常产品。你会如何用“卓越的”和“极好的”这样“有力”和“积极”的语言来描述它？)

6.  **Extension:** You are about to present your new proposal to your boss. What are the three most important points you want to make?
    (你即将向你的老板展示你的新建议书。你最想说明的三个要点是什么？)
