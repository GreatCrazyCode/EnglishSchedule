
# Day 4: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Conducting Market Research (进行市场研究)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **research** (研究)
*   **customer survey** (客户调查)
*   **ask for your opinion** (征求你的意见)
*   **currently use** (目前使用)
*   **in the future** (在未来)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever participated in a "survey"? What was it about?
    (你参加过“调查”吗？是关于什么的？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Why is "doing your homework" or research so important before starting a new project?
    (为什么在开始一个新项目前“做功课”或研究如此重要？)

3.  **Core:** If you wanted to get someone's "opinion" on a new idea, how would you politely ask them?
    (如果你想就一个新想法征求某人的“意见”，你会如何礼貌地询问？)

4.  **Core:** Why is it useful to ask customers what products they "currently use"?
    (为什么询问客户他们“目前使用”什么产品很有用？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine your company wants to open a new coffee shop. What kind of "research" would you do first?
    (想象你的公司想开一家新的咖啡店。你会先做什么样的“研究”？)

6.  **Extension:** You are designing a customer survey. What are some tips for writing good questions that will give you useful information?
    (你正在设计一份客户调查。写出能给你带来有用信息的好问题有哪些技巧？)
