## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **pitch** /pɪtʃ/ - v. 推销，提出（想法）
*   **research** /rɪˈsɜːtʃ/ - n. 研究，调查
*   **survey** /ˈsɜːveɪ/ - n. 调查
*   **opinion** /əˈpɪnjən/ - n. 意见，看法
*   **currently** /ˈkʌrəntli/ - adv. 当前，目前
*   **database** /ˈdeɪtəbeɪs/ - n. 数据库

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **Could I ask for your opinion on a new product?** - 我能请您就一个新产品发表一下意见吗？
    *   *Example:* "Excuse me, **could I ask for your opinion on a new product** we're testing?"
*   **What products do you currently use?** - 您目前使用什么产品？
    *   *Example:* "To understand your needs better, **what products do you currently use** for cleaning?"
*   **What products would you like to see us sell in the future?** - 您希望我们将来销售什么产品？
    *   *Example:* "We value your feedback. **What products would you like to see us sell in the future?**"
*   **How do you feel about the idea of...?** - 您对...的想法感觉如何？
    *   *Example:* "**How do you feel about the idea of** a four-day work week?"
