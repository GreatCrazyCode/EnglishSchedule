## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **appraisal** /əˈpreɪzl/ - n. 评估，评定
*   **review** /rɪˈvjuː/ - v. 回顾，审查
*   **performance** /pəˈfɔːməns/ - n. 表现，业绩
*   **objectives** /əbˈdʒektɪvz/ - n. 目标，目的
*   **compliment** /ˈkɒmplɪmənt/ - n. 赞美，恭维
*   **zest** /zest/ - n. 热情，热忱

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **What has gone well for you this year?** - 今年你有哪些方面做得很好？
    *   *Example:* In my appraisal, my boss asked, "**What has gone well for you this year?**" and I talked about my successful project.
*   **What didn't go so well?** - 哪些方面做得不太好？
    *   *Example:* It's important to be honest when your manager asks, "**What didn't go so well?**"
*   **What should you do more of?** - 你应该多做些什么？
    *   *Example:* My manager suggested I should take more leadership roles when he asked, "**What should you do more of?**"
*   **Let's look ahead and set some objectives.** - 让我们展望未来，设定一些目标。
    *   *Example:* After reviewing the past year, my boss said, "**Let's look ahead and set some objectives** for the next one."
