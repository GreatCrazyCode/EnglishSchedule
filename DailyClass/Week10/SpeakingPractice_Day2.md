
# Day 2: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Health and Safety at Work (工作中的健康与安全)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **accident** (事故)
*   **cause** (原因)
*   **injury** (受伤)
*   **avoid** (避免)
*   **procedure** (程序)
*   **first-aider** (急救员)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** What are some common safety hazards in an office environment?
    (办公室环境中有哪些常见的安全隐患？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever had a small accident at work or school? What happened?
    (你在工作或学校有过小事故吗？发生了什么？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it important to have a clear "procedure" for reporting accidents?
    (为什么为报告事故制定清晰的“程序”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** If someone has an accident, what is the first thing you should do?
    (如果有人发生事故，你首先应该做什么？)

5.  **Extension:** How can a company encourage its employees to be more aware of health and safety?
    (公司如何鼓励员工更加关注健康与安全？)

6.  **Extension:** You are asked to write a short safety guide for new employees. What are the top three safety rules you would include?
    (你被要求为新员工编写一份简短的安全指南。你会包括哪三条最重要的安全规则？)
