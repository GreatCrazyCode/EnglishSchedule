*   **injury** /ˈɪndʒəri/ - n. 受伤，伤害
*   **avoid** /əˈvɔɪd/ - v. 避免，规避

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **How did the accident happen?** - 事故是怎么发生的？
    *   *Example:* "When filling out the report, the first question is always: **How did the accident happen?**"
*   **What was the cause of the accident?** - 事故的原因是什么？
    *   *Example:* "**What was the cause of the accident?** It seems a wet floor was to blame."
*   **Were there any injuries?** - 有人受伤吗？
    *   *Example:* "The fire alarm went off, but thankfully, when the manager asked, '**Were there any injuries?**', the answer was no."
*   **How could the accident have been avoided?** - 这次事故本可以如何避免？
    *   *Example:* "After any incident, it's important to ask: **How could the accident have been avoided?**"
