## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **proposal** /prəˈpəʊzl/ - n. 提议，建议
*   **exceptional** /ɪkˈsepʃənl/ - adj. 杰出的，卓越的
*   **fantastic** /fænˈtæstɪk/ - adj. 极好的，了不起的
*   **quality** /ˈkwɒləti/ - n. 质量，品质
*   **profit** /ˈprɒfɪt/ - n. 利润，收益
*   **efficiency** /ɪˈfɪʃnsi/ - n. 效率
*   **value for money** /ˈvæljuː fə ˈmʌni/ - phrase. 物有所值

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **This exceptional product has fantastic potential.** - 这个卓越的产品具有极好的潜力。
    *   *Example:* "Our new app is easy to use and solves a real problem. **This exceptional product has fantastic potential**."
*   **Demand for this will reach melting point!** - 对此的需求将达到沸点！
    *   *Example:* "Once people see how useful it is, **demand for this will reach melting point!**"
*   **Our customers will get value for money.** - 我们的客户将物有所值。
    *   *Example:* "It's not the cheapest option, but with its long-lasting battery, **our customers will get value for money**."
*   **Our profits will increase.** - 我们的利润将会增加。
    *   *Example:* "By expanding into a new market, we are confident that **our profits will increase**."
