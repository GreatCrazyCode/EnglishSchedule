## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **fussy** /ˈfʌsi/ - adj. 挑剔的，大惊小怪的
*   **availability** /əˌveɪləˈbɪləti/ - n. 可用性，可得性
*   **facilities** /fəˈsɪlətiz/ - n. 设施，设备
*   **reservation** /ˌrezəˈveɪʃn/ - n. 预定，预约
*   **rate** /reɪt/ - n. 价格，费用
*   **include** /ɪnˈkluːd/ - v. 包含，包括
*   **continental breakfast** /ˌkɒntɪˈnentl ˈbrekfəst/ - n. 欧陆式早餐

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I'd like to check availability and prices.** - 我想查询空房情况和价格。
    *   *Example:* "Hello, **I'd like to check availability and prices** for a double room for this weekend."
*   **Does the price include breakfast?** - 这个价格包含早餐吗？
    *   *Example:* "Before I book, can you tell me **if the price includes breakfast**?"
*   **Are there any business facilities?** - 有商务设施吗？
    *   *Example:* "We need to work during our stay, so **are there any business facilities** like a meeting room?"
*   **I'd like to go ahead and make a reservation.** - 我想继续并进行预订。
    *   *Example:* "Everything sounds good. **I'd like to go ahead and make a reservation** for two nights."
