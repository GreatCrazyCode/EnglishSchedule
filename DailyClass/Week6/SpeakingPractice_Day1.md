
# Day 1: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Handling Emergencies at Work (应对工作中的紧急情况)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **evacuate** (疏散)
*   **fire drill** (消防演习)
*   **take charge** (负责)
*   **panic** (恐慌)
*   **emergency** (紧急情况)
*   **There's no need to panic.** (没必要恐慌。)
*   **Use the stairs, not the lift.** (请走楼梯，不要乘电梯。)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever participated in a fire drill? What was it like? 
    (你参加过消防演习吗？感觉怎么样？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What are some common emergencies that can happen in an office?
    (办公室里可能发生哪些常见的紧急情况？)

3.  **Core:** If you hear a fire alarm at work, what is the first thing you should do?
    (如果在工作中听到火警，你首先应该做什么？)

4.  **Core:** Why is it important to "use the stairs, not the lift" during a fire?
    (为什么在火灾中要“走楼梯，不要乘电梯”？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you have to "take charge" during an emergency. What kind of instructions would you give to your colleagues?
    (想象一下，在紧急情况下你需要“负责”。你会给同事下达什么样的指示？)

6.  **Extension:** How can a company help its employees feel prepared for emergencies instead of feeling panic?
    (公司如何帮助员工为紧急情况做好准备，而不是感到恐慌？)
