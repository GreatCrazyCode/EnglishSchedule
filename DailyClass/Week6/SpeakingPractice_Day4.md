
# Day 4: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Delivering Bad News (传达坏消息)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **give it to you straight** (直说)
*   **the outlook is gloomy** (前景黯淡)
*   **profit warning** (盈利预警)
*   **redundancies** (裁员)
*   **on the line** (处于危险中)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever had to give someone bad news? How did you feel?
    (你曾不得不告诉别人坏消息吗？你感觉如何？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Why is it difficult to deliver bad news in a professional setting?
    (为什么在专业场合传达坏消息很困难？)

3.  **Core:** When delivering bad news, why is it important to "give it to you straight"?
    (在传达坏消息时，为什么“直说”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** If you hear that "the outlook is gloomy" for your company, what would be your main concerns?
    (如果你听说公司的“前景黯淡”，你主要会担心什么？)

5.  **Extension:** If your manager says your job is "on the line," what steps would you take to improve the situation?
    (如果你的经理说你的工作“岌岌可危”，你会采取什么措施来改善情况？)

6.  **Extension:** Some people prefer to be told bad news directly, while others prefer a softer approach. What is your preference and why?
    (有些人喜欢直接被告知坏消息，而另一些人则喜欢更委婉的方式。你更喜欢哪种方式，为什么？)
