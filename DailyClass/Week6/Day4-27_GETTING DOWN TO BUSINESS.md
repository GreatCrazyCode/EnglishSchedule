
# Day 4: 27. GETTING DOWN TO BUSINESS

## 1. Link to Material (学习资料)

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **gloomy** /ˈɡluːmi/ - adj. 悲观的，前景黯淡的
*   **recession** /rɪˈseʃn/ - n. 经济衰退
*   **meltdown** /ˈmeltdaʊn/ - n. 崩溃，瓦解
*   **sector** /ˈsektə(r)/ - n. 行业，领域
*   **profit warning** /ˈprɒfɪt ˈwɔːnɪŋ/ - n. 盈利预警
*   **redundancies** /rɪˈdʌndənsiz/ - n. 裁员
*   **on the line** /ɒn ðə laɪn/ - phrase. 处于危险中

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I've got to give it to you straight.** - 我得跟你直说。
    *   *Example:* "Look, **I've got to give it to you straight** – the project is behind schedule."
*   **The outlook is gloomy.** - 前景不容乐观。
    *   *Example:* "With the recent sales figures, **the outlook is gloomy** for the next quarter."
*   **I've got to announce a profit warning.** - 我不得不宣布一个盈利预警。
    *   *Example:* "Due to unexpected costs, **I've got to announce a profit warning** to our investors."
*   **Your necks will be on the line.** - 你们的职位将岌岌可危。
    *   *Example:* "If this launch fails, **your necks will be on the line**."
