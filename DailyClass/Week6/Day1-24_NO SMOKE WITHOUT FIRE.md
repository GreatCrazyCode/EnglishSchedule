
# Day 1: 24. NO SMOKE WITHOUT FIRE

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **evacuate** /ɪˈvækjueɪt/ - v. 疏散，撤离
*   **fire drill** /ˈfaɪə drɪl/ - n. 消防演习
*   **take charge** /teɪk tʃɑːdʒ/ - phrase. 掌管，负责
*   **panic** /ˈpænɪk/ - n./v. 恐慌
*   **missing** /ˈmɪsɪŋ/ - adj. 失踪的，不见的
*   **emergency** /ɪˈmɜːdʒənsi/ - n. 紧急情况
*   **stairs** /steəz/ - n. 楼梯

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **There's no need to panic.** - 没必要恐慌。
    *   *Example:* Even if the alarm goes off, **there's no need to panic**; it might just be a drill.
*   **Please leave the building.** - 请离开大楼。
    *   *Example:* When you hear the alarm, **please leave the building** immediately.
*   **Use the stairs, not the lift.** - 请走楼梯，不要乘电梯。
    *   *Example:* In case of a fire, always **use the stairs, not the lift**.
*   **Is anybody missing?** - 有人失踪吗？
    *   *Example:* After we evacuated, the manager asked, "**Is anybody missing?**"
