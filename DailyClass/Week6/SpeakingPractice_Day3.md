
# Day 3: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Making a Complaint (进行投诉)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **disappointed** (失望的)
*   **standard of service** (服务标准)
*   **meet my expectations** (达到我的期望)
*   **resolve the matter** (解决问题)
*   **get a full refund** (获得全额退款)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Are you good at complaining when you are unhappy with a product or service? Why or why not?
    (当你对产品或服务不满意时，你擅长投诉吗？为什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What is the most common thing people complain about in a hotel?
    (在酒店里，人们最常抱怨的是什么？)

3.  **Core:** How can you complain politely without getting angry?
    (你如何能礼貌地投诉而又不生气？)

4.  **Core:** If a hotel room "did not meet your expectations," what would you say to the receptionist?
    (如果酒店房间“没有达到你的期望”，你会对接待员说什么？)

5.  **Extension:** What is the difference between asking to "resolve the matter" and asking for a "full refund"? In what situations would you ask for each?
    (要求“解决问题”和要求“全额退款”有什么区别？在什么情况下你会分别提出这两种要求？)

6.  **Extension:** Do you think complaining is always effective? What makes a complaint successful?
    (你认为投诉总是有效的吗？什么因素使投诉成功？)
