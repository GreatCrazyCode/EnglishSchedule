
# Day 3: 26. YOU CANNOT BE SERIOUS!

## 1. Link to Material (学习资料)

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **disappointed** /ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪntɪd/ - adj. 失望的
*   **standard** /ˈstændəd/ - n. 标准，水平
*   **expectation** /ˌekspekˈteɪʃn/ - n. 期望，预期
*   **resolve** /rɪˈzɒlv/ - v. 解决，处理
*   **matter** /ˈmætə(r)/ - n. 事情，问题
*   **cancel** /ˈkænsl/ - v. 取消
*   **refund** /ˈriːfʌnd/ - n. 退款

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I'm very disappointed with your service.** - 我对你们的服务非常失望。
    *   *Example:* "**I'm very disappointed with your service**; the package arrived damaged."
*   **The standard of service is not good enough.** - 服务水平不够好。
    *   *Example:* "We waited for an hour to be seated. **The standard of service is not good enough.**"
*   **This did not meet my expectations.** - 这没有达到我的期望。
    *   *Example:* "The product description was misleading; **this did not meet my expectations** at all."
*   **I would like this matter resolved as quickly as possible.** - 我希望这个问题能尽快得到解决。
    *   *Example:* "My order is incorrect. **I would like this matter resolved as quickly as possible.**"
