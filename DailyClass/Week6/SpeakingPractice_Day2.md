
# Day 2: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Booking a Hotel for a Business Trip (为商务旅行预订酒店)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **check availability** (查询可用性)
*   **rate** (价格)
*   **facilities** (设施)
*   **make a reservation** (进行预订)
*   **include breakfast** (包含早餐)
*   **fussy** (挑剔的)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** When you travel, what is the most important thing you look for in a hotel? 
    (你旅行时，在酒店中最看重的是什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever booked a hotel over the phone? Was it easy or difficult?
    (你试过电话预订酒店吗？容易还是困难？)

3.  **Core:** What information do you need to provide when you "make a reservation"?
    (当你“进行预订”时，需要提供哪些信息？)

4.  **Core:** Imagine you are booking a hotel. How would you ask if the hotel has a gym and a swimming pool?
    (想象一下你正在预订酒店。你会如何询问酒店是否有健身房和游泳池？)

5.  **Extension:** Your boss is very "fussy" about food. How would you ask the hotel about their breakfast options to make sure your boss is happy?
    (你的老板对食物非常“挑剔”。你会如何询问酒店的早餐选择以确保你的老板满意？)

6.  **Extension:** What would you do if you arrived at a hotel and the room was not what you expected?
    (如果你到达酒店后发现房间不符合你的预期，你会怎么做？)
