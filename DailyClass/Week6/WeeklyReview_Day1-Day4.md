
# Week 6 Review: Day 1 - Day 4

## Introduction (引言)
This week, we focused on handling difficult and high-pressure situations at work. From managing emergencies and dealing with complaints to delivering bad news, we've learned essential language to communicate clearly and professionally under stress.
本周，我们专注于处理工作中的困难和高压情况。从管理紧急情况、处理投诉到传达坏消息，我们学习了在压力下清晰、专业地进行沟通的基本语言。

## Topic Review (主题回顾)
*   **Day 1: Handling Emergencies (处理紧急情况)**
    *   We learned how to give clear and calm instructions during an emergency, such as a fire alarm.
    *   我们学习了如何在火警等紧急情况下，清晰、冷静地发出指示。

*   **Day 2: Booking for a "Fussy" Client (为“挑剔”的客户预订)**
    *   We practiced the language for booking a hotel, focusing on asking specific questions to ensure a client's particular needs are met.
    *   我们练习了预订酒店的用语，重点是如何提出具体问题以确保满足客户的特殊需求。

*   **Day 3: Making a Complaint (进行投诉)**
    *   We focused on how to complain politely but firmly when a product or service does not meet our expectations.
    *   我们专注于在产品或服务不符合期望时，如何礼貌而坚定地进行投诉。

*   **Day 4: Delivering Bad News (传达坏消息)**
    *   We learned how to deliver bad news directly and professionally, using phrases to be honest about a difficult situation.
    *   我们学习了如何直接且专业地传达坏消息，使用坦率的短语来说明困难的处境。

## Core Vocabulary and Expressions (核心词汇与表达式)

### Emergency & Safety (紧急与安全)
*   **Vocabulary:** evacuate, fire drill, take charge, panic, emergency, stairs, on the line
*   **Expressions:** 
    *   There's no need to panic.
    *   Use the stairs, not the lift.
    *   Is anybody missing?

### Booking & Complaining (预订与投诉)
*   **Vocabulary:** fussy, availability, facilities, reservation, rate, include, disappointed, standard, expectation, resolve, matter, cancel, refund
*   **Expressions:**
    *   I'd like to check availability and prices.
    *   Does the price include breakfast?
    *   I'm very disappointed with your service.
    *   This did not meet my expectations.

### Delivering Bad News (传达坏消息)
*   **Vocabulary:** gloomy, recession, meltdown, sector, profit warning, redundancies
*   **Expressions:**
    *   I've got to give it to you straight.
    *   The outlook is gloomy.
    *   Your necks will be on the line.

## Comprehensive Exercise (综合练习)
**Scenario:** You are a manager at a hotel. A very important guest is complaining that their room is too small and the Wi-Fi is not working. At the same time, your company is facing a financial "recession" and you need to inform your staff about potential cutbacks.

**Task:** Write a short dialogue where you first handle the guest's complaint, and then briefly speak to your staff about the "gloomy" outlook. Use at least **4** of the key words or expressions from this week.

---
**(情景对话):** 你是一家酒店的经理。一位非常重要的客人投诉说他们的房间太小，而且Wi-Fi无法使用。与此同时，你的公司正面临财务“衰退”，你需要告知你的员工可能要削减开支。

**任务:** 写一段简短的对话。在对话中，你首先处理客人的投诉，然后简要地向你的员工说明“黯淡”的前景。请至少使用本周学到的 **4个** 核心词汇或表达式。
