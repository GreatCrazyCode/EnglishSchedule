
# Day 2: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Booking a Flight (预订机票)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **outbound and return date** (往返日期)
*   **business or economy** (商务舱或经济舱)
*   **fixed or flexible ticket** (固定或灵活机票)
*   **window or aisle seat** (靠窗或靠过道座位)
*   **frequent flyer number** (常旅客号码)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** How do you usually book your flights? Online, or through a travel agent?
    (你通常如何预订机票？在线还是通过旅行社？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Do you prefer a window or an aisle seat? Why?
    (你更喜欢靠窗还是靠过道的座位？为什么？)

3.  **Core:** What is the difference between a "fixed" and a "flexible" ticket? When would you choose a flexible ticket?
    (“固定”机票和“灵活”机票有什么区别？你什么时候会选择灵活机票？)

4.  **Core:** What are the benefits of having a "frequent flyer number"?
    (拥有“常旅客号码”有什么好处？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you are a travel agent. A customer wants to book a flight to Paris for a holiday. What questions would you ask them?
    (想象你是一名旅行社代理。一位顾客想预订去巴黎度假的机票。你会问他们什么问题？)

6.  **Extension:** What are some tips for finding cheap flights?
    (找便宜机票有什么诀窍吗？)
