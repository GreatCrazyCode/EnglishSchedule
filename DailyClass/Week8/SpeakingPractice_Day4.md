
# Day 4: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Reporting to Your Manager (向你的经理汇报)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **predicament** (困境)
*   **brief your manager** (向经理汇报)
*   **keep someone in the picture** (让某人知情)
*   **close the deal** (达成交易)
*   **proceed with the order** (处理订单)
*   **committed to** (致力于)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** How often do you "keep your boss in the picture" about your work?
    (你多久向你的老板汇报一次你的工作进展？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever been in a "predicament" while travelling? What happened?
    (你在旅行中遇到过“困境”吗？发生了什么？)

3.  **Core:** When you have good news, like "closing a deal," what is the best way to share it with your team?
    (当你有好消息，比如“达成交易”时，与团队分享的最佳方式是什么？)

4.  **Core:** What does it mean to be "committed to" something? Can you give an example from your own life?
    (“致力于”某事是什么意思？你能从你自己的生活中举个例子吗？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine your flight for an important business trip is "cancelled". What steps would you take immediately? Who would you inform?
    (想象一下，你一次重要商务旅行的航班被“取消”了。你会立即采取什么措施？你会通知谁？)

6.  **Extension:** When you "brief your manager" about a project, what are the most important pieces of information you should include?
    (当你向你的经理“汇报”一个项目时，你应该包括哪些最重要的信息？)
