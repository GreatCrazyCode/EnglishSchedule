
# Day 1: 32. THE DEAL IS OFF!

## 1. Link to Material (学习资料)
*   [33. THE DEAL IS OFF!](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/english-at-work/32-negotiating-a-deal-2)

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **ripped off** /rɪpt ɒf/ - phrase. 被敲竹杠，被宰
*   **renegotiate** /ˌriːnəˈɡəʊʃieɪt/ - v. 重新谈判
*   **valued customer** /ˈvæljuːd ˈkʌstəmə(r)/ - n. 尊贵的客户
*   **competitor** /kəmˈpetɪtə(r)/ - n. 竞争者
*   **match the price** /mætʃ ðə praɪs/ - phrase. 匹配价格
*   **undercut** /ˌʌndəˈkʌt/ - v. 削价竞争

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I'm sorry to hear you're not happy with our price.** - 我很遗憾听到您对我们的价格不满意。
    *   *Example:* "**I'm sorry to hear you're not happy with our price.** Let's see if we can find a solution."
*   **You are a valued customer and your business is very important to us.** - 您是我们的尊贵客户，您的业务对我们非常重要。
    *   *Example:* "Before we discuss the issue, I want you to know that **you are a valued customer and your business is very important to us**."
*   **We can match the price of our competitors.** - 我们可以匹配我们竞争对手的价格。
    *   *Example:* "If you show us a lower offer, **we can match the price of our competitors**."
