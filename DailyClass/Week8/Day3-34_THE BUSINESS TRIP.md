
# Day 3: 34. THE BUSINESS TRIP

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **convince** /kənˈvɪns/ - v. 说服，使确信
*   **guarantee** /ˌɡærənˈtiː/ - n. 保证，担保
*   **disappointed** /ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪntɪd/ - adj. 失望的
*   **contract** /ˈkɒntrækt/ - n. 合同
*   **pleasure** /ˈpleʒə(r)/ - n. 荣幸，愉快
*   **colleague** /ˈkɒliːɡ/ - n. 同事

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **Thank you for sparing the time to see us.** - 感谢您抽出时间见我们。
    *   *Example:* "Good morning, Mr. Smith. **Thank you for sparing the time to see us** on such short notice."
*   **Each product comes with a satisfaction guarantee.** - 每件产品都有满意度保证。
    *   *Example:* "You can buy with confidence. **Each product comes with a satisfaction guarantee**."
*   **I would be grateful if you could just sign the contract.** - 如果您能签署这份合同，我将不胜感激。
    *   *Example:* "Everything seems to be in order. **I would be grateful if you could just sign the contract**."
*   **It was a pleasure doing business with you.** - 与您合作很愉快。
    *   *Example:* "The project is complete. **It was a pleasure doing business with you**."
