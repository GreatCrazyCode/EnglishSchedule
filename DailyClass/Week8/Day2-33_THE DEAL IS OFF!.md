
# Day 2: 33. THE DEAL IS OFF!

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **clinch the deal** /klɪntʃ ðə diːl/ - phrase. 达成交易
*   **outbound** /aʊtbaʊnd/ - adj. 出境的，向外的
*   **return** /rɪˈtɜːn/ - adj. 返回的
*   **economy class** /ɪˈkɒnəmi klɑːs/ - n. 经济舱
*   **flexible ticket** /ˈfleksəbl ˈtɪkɪt/ - n. 灵活机票
*   **aisle seat** /aɪl siːt/ - n. 靠过道的座位
*   **frequent flyer** /ˈfriːkwənt ˈflaɪə(r)/ - n. 经常乘飞机的旅客

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **What is the outbound and return date?** - 出发和返回日期是什么时候？
    *   *Example:* "To book your flight, I need to know: **what is the outbound and return date?**"
*   **Do you want to fly business or economy?** - 你想坐商务舱还是经济舱？
    *   *Example:* "**Do you want to fly business or economy?** Business class is more expensive but more comfortable."
*   **Do you want a fixed or flexible ticket?** - 你想要固定机票还是灵活机票？
    *   *Example:* "**Do you want a fixed or flexible ticket?** A flexible one allows you to change your flight date."
*   **Would you like a window or aisle seat?** - 你想要靠窗还是靠过道的座位？
    *   *Example:* "For your comfort, **would you like a window or aisle seat?**"
