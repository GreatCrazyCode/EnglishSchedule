
# Day 4: 35. LOST IN FRANCE

## 1. Link to Material (学习资料)

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **predicament** /prɪˈdɪkəmənt/ - n. 困境，窘境
*   **cancelled** /ˈkænsld/ - adj. 取消的
*   **brief** /briːf/ - v. 向...介绍情况，做...的摘要
*   **keep someone in the picture** - phrase. 让某人了解最新情况
*   **proceed** /prəˈsiːd/ - v. 继续进行
*   **committed** /kəˈmɪtɪd/ - adj. 尽心尽力的，坚定的

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **It's good news – I've closed the deal.** - 是个好消息 - 我已经达成了交易。
    *   *Example:* "I called the client and **it's good news - I've closed the deal**!"
*   **We need to proceed with the order as quickly as possible.** - 我们需要尽快处理订单。
    *   *Example:* "Now that the contract is signed, **we need to proceed with the order as quickly as possible**."
*   **I'm committed to getting the order delivered on time.** - 我保证会按时交付订单。
    *   *Example:* "Don't worry about the deadline. **I'm committed to getting the order delivered on time**."

## 4. Exercises (练习)
1.  **Fill in the blank (填空题):**
    *   Even though there were some problems, we are \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ to finishing the project successfully.
    *   尽管存在一些问题，我们仍致力于成功完成该项目。

2.  **Situational Question (情景问答):**
