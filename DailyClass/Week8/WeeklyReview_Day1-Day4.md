
# Week 8 Review: Day 1 - Day 4

## Introduction (引言)
This week, we focused on the lifecycle of a business deal, from renegotiating terms and booking travel to closing the deal and reporting back. We've learned how to handle customer objections, manage business travel, and communicate progress effectively to a manager.
本周，我们专注于商务交易的整个生命周期，从重新谈判条款、预订差旅，到完成交易和汇报工作。我们学习了如何处理客户异议、管理商务差旅，以及如何有效地向经理沟通进度。

## Topic Review (主题回顾)
*   **Day 1: Saving a Deal (挽救交易)**
    *   We learned how to save a deal by reassuring a "valued customer" and offering to match a competitor's price.
    *   我们学习了如何通过安抚“尊贵客户”并提出匹配竞争对手价格来挽救一笔交易。

*   **Day 2: Booking a Flight (预订机票)**
    *   We practiced the essential questions and vocabulary needed to book a flight for a business trip.
    *   我们练习了为商务旅行预订机票所需的基本问题和词汇。

*   **Day 3: Clinching a Deal (达成交易)**
    *   We focused on the final, face-to-face steps of a negotiation and the polite language used to get a contract signed.
    *   我们专注于谈判的最后面对面步骤，以及为使合同签署所使用的礼貌用语。

*   **Day 4: Reporting Progress (汇报进度)**
    *   We learned how to "brief" a manager on our progress and how to handle unexpected travel problems like a cancelled flight.
    *   我们学习了如何向经理“汇报”我们的进展，以及如何处理像航班取消这样的意外差旅问题。

## Core Vocabulary and Expressions (核心词汇与表达式)

### Negotiation & Deals (谈判与交易)
*   **Vocabulary:** ripped off, renegotiate, valued customer, competitor, match the price, clinch the deal, contract, close the deal
*   **Expressions:** 
    *   I'm sorry to hear you're not happy with our price.
    *   You are a valued customer and your business is very important to us.
    *   We can match the price of our competitors.
    *   It was a pleasure doing business with you.

### Travel (差旅)
*   **Vocabulary:** outbound, return, economy class, flexible ticket, aisle seat, frequent flyer, cancelled
*   **Expressions:**
    *   What is the outbound and return date?
    *   Do you want a fixed or flexible ticket?
    *   Would you like a window or aisle seat?

### Reporting & Commitment (汇报与承诺)
*   **Vocabulary:** brief, predicament, proceed, committed
*   **Expressions:**
    *   keep someone in the picture
    *   I've closed the deal.
    *   I'm committed to getting the order delivered on time.

## Comprehensive Exercise (综合练习)
**Scenario:** You have just returned from a successful business trip where you "clinched a deal". However, your "return" flight was "cancelled", and you had to stay an extra night. Now, you need to "brief" your manager.

**Task:** Write a short email to your manager. Tell them the good news about the deal and briefly explain the travel "predicament". Use at least **4** of the key words or expressions from this week.

---
**(情景练习):** 你刚刚从一次成功的商务旅行回来，并“达成了一笔交易”。然而，你的“返程”航班被“取消”了，你不得不多住一晚。现在，你需要向你的经理“汇报情况”。

**任务:** 给你的经理写一封简短的电子邮件。告诉他们关于交易的好消息，并简要解释旅行中遇到的“困境”。请至少使用本周学到的 **4个** 核心词汇或表达式。
