
# Day 1: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Saving a Business Deal (挽救一笔生意)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **ripped off** (被敲竹杠)
*   **renegotiate** (重新谈判)
*   **valued customer** (尊贵的客户)
*   **match the price** (匹配价格)
*   **competitor** (竞争者)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever felt "ripped off" when buying something? What happened?
    (你买东西时有没有感觉被“敲竹杠”？发生了什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What do you think is the most important quality of a good salesperson?
    (你认为一个好的销售人员最重要的品质是什么？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it important to tell a client they are a "valued customer"?
    (为什么告诉客户他们是“尊贵的客户”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** If a competitor is offering a lower price, what are some ways a company can still keep its customers?
    (如果一个竞争对手提供更低的价格，公司有哪些方法可以留住客户？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you are a customer who is unhappy with a service. What would you expect the company to do to fix the situation?
    (想象你是一个对服务不满意的顾客。你期望公司做什么来解决问题？)

6.  **Extension:** Is it always a good idea for a company to "match the price" of its competitors? What are the potential risks?
    (对于一家公司来说，“匹配其竞争对手的价格”总是一个好主意吗？潜在的风险是什么？)
