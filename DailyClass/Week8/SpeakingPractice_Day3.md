
# Day 3: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Closing a Business Deal (完成一笔生意)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **spare the time** (抽出时间)
*   **satisfaction guarantee** (满意度保证)
*   **sign the contract** (签署合同)
*   **pleasure doing business** (合作愉快)
*   **convince** (说服)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** What is the most important part of a business meeting for you?
    (对你来说，商务会议最重要的部分是什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** How do you feel when you successfully "convince" someone to agree with you?
    (当你成功“说服”某人同意你的观点时，你感觉如何？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it polite to say "Thank you for sparing the time to see us"?
    (为什么说“感谢您抽出时间见我们”是礼貌的？)

4.  **Core:** What does a "satisfaction guarantee" mean to you as a customer?
    (作为一名顾客，“满意度保证”对你意味着什么？)

5.  **Extension:** You are at the end of a successful negotiation. What are the final steps to "clinch the deal"?
    (你正处于一次成功谈判的尾声。要“达成交易”的最后步骤是什么？)

6.  **Extension:** After you "sign the contract," what are the most important things to do to maintain a good relationship with the client?
    (在你“签署合同”之后，为了与客户保持良好关系，最重要的事情是什么？)
