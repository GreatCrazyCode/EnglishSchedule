
# Day 1: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Working with a New Colleague (与新同事共事)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **work alongside** (与...一起工作)
*   **in charge** (负责)
*   **awkward** (尴尬的)
*   **plan of action** (行动计划)
*   **respect my decision** (尊重我的决定)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** What are some challenges you might face when working with a new person?
    (与新人共事时，你可能会遇到哪些挑战？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What is the best way to welcome a new colleague to the team?
    (欢迎新同事加入团队的最好方式是什么？)

3.  **Core:** If you and a colleague disagree on a "plan of action," what is the best way to resolve it?
    (如果你和同事在“行动计划”上意见不合，最好的解决方法是什么？)

4.  **Core:** Why is it important to say "I understand you have lots of experience" when talking to a new, senior colleague?
    (在与一位资深新同事交谈时，为什么说“我知道您经验丰富”很重要？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you are "in charge" of a project, but a new team member keeps challenging your decisions. How would you handle the situation?
    (想象一下，你“负责”一个项目，但一个新团队成员不断挑战你的决定。你会如何处理这种情况？)

6.  **Extension:** What are the key ingredients for a successful working relationship?
    (成功的合作关系的关键要素是什么？)
