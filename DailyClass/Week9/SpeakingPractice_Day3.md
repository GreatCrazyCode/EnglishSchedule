
# Day 3: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Building Good Customer Relations (建立良好的客户关系)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **customer relations** (客户关系)
*   **salvage** (挽救)
*   **pleased with our service** (对我们的服务满意)
*   **follow up** (跟进)
*   **happy with the order** (对订单满意)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Think of a time you received really good customer service. What made it good?
    (想一次你得到过非常好的客户服务的经历。是什么让它变得好？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Why is it important for a company to have good "customer relations"?
    (为什么公司拥有良好的“客户关系”很重要？)

3.  **Core:** What is a "follow-up call"? Why is it a good idea to make one after a sale?
    (什么是“跟进电话”？为什么在销售后打一个跟进电话是个好主意？)

4.  **Core:** When you ask a customer, "Do you have any questions?", what does it show?
    (当你问客户“你有什么问题吗？”时，这表明了什么？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine a customer received the wrong product. How would you "salvage" the situation and keep them as a customer?
    (想象一个客户收到了错误的产品。你会如何“挽救”局面并留住他们作为客户？)

6.  **Extension:** What are some other ways, besides phone calls, to build strong customer relations?
    (除了打电话，还有哪些其他方法可以建立牢固的客户关系？)
