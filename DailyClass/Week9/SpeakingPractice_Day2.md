
# Day 2: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Dealing with a Difficult Colleague (与难相处的同事打交道)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **concerned** (担心的)
*   **affect the business** (影响业务)
*   **not pulling her weight** (没有尽职尽责)
*   **difficult to work with** (难以共事)
*   **refer up** (上报)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever had a colleague who was "difficult to work with"? What made them difficult?
    (你曾有过“难以共事”的同事吗？是什么让他们变得困难？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What is the difference between a helpful colleague and one who is a "hindrance"?
    (一个有帮助的同事和一个“障碍”之间有什么区别？)

3.  **Core:** When is it a good idea to "refer up" a problem to your manager?
    (什么时候将问题“上报”给你的经理是个好主意？)

4.  **Core:** If you are "concerned that a colleague has made a mistake," what is the most professional way to handle it?
    (如果你“担心同事犯了错误”，最专业的处理方式是什么？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine a team member is "not pulling their weight." Would you talk to them directly first, or go straight to your manager? Why?
    (想象一个团队成员“没有尽职尽责”。你会先直接和他们谈，还是直接去找你的经理？为什么？)

6.  **Extension:** What are some strategies for building a better relationship with a colleague you find difficult?
    (与你觉得难相处的同事建立更好关系有哪些策略？)
