## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **tense** /tens/ - adj. 紧张的
*   **incompetence** /ɪnˈkɒmpɪtəns/ - n. 无能，不胜任
*   **salvage** /ˈsælvɪdʒ/ - v. 挽救，挽回
*   **customer relations** /ˈkʌstəmə rɪˈleɪʃnz/ - n. 客户关系
*   **interfere** /ˌɪntəˈfɪə(r)/ - v. 干涉，干预
*   **pleased** /pliːzd/ - adj. 高兴的，满意的

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **How are you today?** - 您今天好吗？
    *   *Example:* "Good morning, this is Sarah calling from TechCorp. **How are you today?**"
*   **I hope you have received our order and are happy with it.** - 希望您已收到我们的订单并对此感到满意。
    *   *Example:* "I'm just calling to follow up. **I hope you have received our order and are happy with it.**"
*   **We do hope you are pleased with our service.** - 我们真诚地希望您对我们的服务感到满意。
    *   *Example:* "Your satisfaction is our priority. **We do hope you are pleased with our service.**"
*   **Do you have any questions you may want to ask?** - 您有什么问题想问吗？
    *   *Example:* "That's all from my side. **Do you have any questions you may want to ask?**"
