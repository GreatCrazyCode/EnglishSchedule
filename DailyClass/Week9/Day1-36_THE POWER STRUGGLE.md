## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **grumpy** /ˈɡrʌmpi/ - adj. 脾气坏的，易怒的
*   **alongside** /əˌlɒŋˈsaɪd/ - prep. 在...旁边，与...一起
*   **in charge** /ɪn tʃɑːdʒ/ - phrase. 负责，主管
*   **awkward** /ˈɔːkwəd/ - adj. 尴尬的，棘手的
*   **enthusiasm** /ɪnˈθjuːziæzəm/ - n. 热情，热心
*   **respect** /rɪˈspekt/ - v. 尊重，尊敬

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I understand you have lots of experience.** - 我知道您经验丰富。
    *   *Example:* "**I understand you have lots of experience**, so I'd value your opinion on this."
*   **Together we can do this job really well.** - 我们一起可以把这项工作做得很好。
    *   *Example:* "You have great design skills and I'm good at marketing. **Together we can do this job really well**."
*   **Let's discuss this so we can agree on a plan of action.** - 让我们讨论一下，以便我们能商定一个行动计划。
    *   *Example:* "We have different ideas. **Let's discuss this so we can agree on a plan of action**."
*   **Please respect my decision and let's move on.** - 请尊重我的决定，让我们继续前进。
    *   *Example:* "I've made my choice. **Please respect my decision and let's move on** to the next topic."
