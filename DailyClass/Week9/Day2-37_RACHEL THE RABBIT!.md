## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **struggling** /ˈstrʌɡlɪŋ/ - adj. 挣扎的，奋斗的
*   **hinder** /ˈhɪndə(r)/ - v. 阻碍，妨碍
*   **concerned** /kənˈsɜːnd/ - adj. 担心的，关心的
*   **affect** /əˈfekt/ - v. 影响
*   **pull her weight** /pʊl hɜː weɪt/ - phrase. 尽职尽责，努力工作
*   **refer up** /rɪˈfɜːr ʌp/ - phrase. 上报，请示上级

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I'm concerned that she's made a mistake.** - 我担心她犯了一个错误。
    *   *Example:* "**I'm concerned that she's made a mistake** on the invoice; the numbers don't add up."
*   **This might affect the business.** - 这可能会影响到业务。
    *   *Example:* "We need to fix this issue quickly. **This might affect the business** if clients find out."
*   **She's not pulling her weight.** - 她没有尽职尽责。
    *   *Example:* "The whole team is working late, but **she's not pulling her weight**."
*   **I'm finding it very difficult to work with her.** - 我发现和她一起工作非常困难。
    *   *Example:* "We have completely different ideas. **I'm finding it very difficult to work with her**."

    *   如果我们想按时完成项目，团队中的每个人都需要尽职尽责。
