
# Week 9 Review: Day 1 - Day 4

## Introduction (引言)
This week, we focused on the challenging but essential skills of managing people and client relationships. We learned how to work with new colleagues, handle difficult staff, maintain good customer relations, and take disciplinary action when necessary.
本周，我们专注于管理人员和客户关系这些具有挑战性但至关重要的技能。我们学习了如何与新同事合作，处理难相处的员工，维护良好的客户关系，以及在必要时采取纪律处分。

## Topic Review (主题回顾)
*   **Day 1: Working with a New Colleague (与新同事共事)**
    *   We learned phrases to build a working relationship with a new colleague, even when there is a "power struggle".
    *   我们学习了一些短语，以便在即使存在“权力斗争”的情况下，也能与新同事建立工作关系。

*   **Day 2: Reporting a Difficult Colleague (报告难相处的同事)**
    *   We practiced how to professionally report a colleague who is "not pulling their weight" to a manager.
    *   我们练习了如何专业地向经理报告一个“不尽职”的同事。

*   **Day 3: Following Up with a Client (跟进客户)**
    *   We focused on the importance of follow-up calls to maintain good "customer relations".
    *   我们专注于通过后续电话来维护良好“客户关系”的重要性。

*   **Day 4: Disciplining a Staff Member (纪律处分员工)**
    *   We learned the formal language for giving a "verbal warning" and taking "disciplinary action".
    *   我们学习了发出“口头警告”和采取“纪律处分”的正式语言。

## Core Vocabulary and Expressions (核心词汇与表达式)

### Teamwork & Colleagues (团队合作与同事)
*   **Vocabulary:** work alongside, in charge, awkward, enthusiasm, respect, struggling, hinder, concerned, affect, pull her weight, refer up
*   **Expressions:** 
    *   Together we can do this job really well.
    *   Let's discuss this so we can agree on a plan of action.
    *   I'm finding it very difficult to work with her.

### Customer Relations (客户关系)
*   **Vocabulary:** salvage, customer relations, pleased
*   **Expressions:**
    *   I hope you have received our order and are happy with it.
    *   We do hope you are pleased with our service.

### Discipline & Mistakes (纪律与错误)
*   **Vocabulary:** discipline, consult, verbal warning, disciplinary action, serious mistakes, mess up
*   **Expressions:**
    *   You've made some serious mistakes.
    *   You need to consult me before...
    *   These mistakes cannot happen again.

## Comprehensive Exercise (综合练习)
**Scenario:** You are a team leader. A new team member, Alex, has made a "serious mistake" that has upset a client. First, you need to call the client to "salvage" the relationship. Then, you need to speak with Alex to give him a "verbal warning".

**Task:** Write a short plan of what you would say in the phone call to the client, and then what you would say to Alex. Use at least **4** of the key words or expressions from this week.

---
**(情景练习):** 你是一位团队负责人。新团队成员Alex犯了一个“严重的错误”，让一位客户感到不安。首先，你需要打电话给客户以“挽救”关系。然后，你需要与Alex谈话，给他一个“口头警告”。

**任务:** 写一个简短的计划，说明你会在给客户的电话中说什么，然后你会对Alex说什么。请至少使用本周学到的 **4个** 关键词或表达式。
