
# 口语练习 (Speaking Practice) - Day 3 & 4 (Week 4)

## 话题 (Topic)
Placing orders and writing professional business emails.
(下订单与撰写专业的商务邮件)

---

## 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)
*   **place an order**: 下订单
*   **in stock**: 有库存
*   **confirm**: 确认
*   **appropriate**: 适当的，合适的
*   **regarding**: 关于，至于
*   **text-speak**: 短信语言，网络用语
*   **I'd like to place an order for...**: 我想订购...
*   **We're going to need...**: 我们将需要...
*   **Could you send...**: 你能寄送...吗？
*   **When can we expect to receive them?**: 我们什么时候能收到它们？
*   **Dear Mr/Ms [Last Name]...**: 尊敬的[姓氏]先生/女士...
*   **I hope you are well.**: 希望您一切安好。
*   **I am writing regarding...**: 我写信是关于...
*   **Please could you confirm...**: 请您确认...

---

## 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

1.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   Have you ever placed an order for something important, either for personal use or for work? What was the process like?
    *   (你有没有为个人或工作订购过重要的东西？过程是怎样的？)

2.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   How often do you write business emails? What do you think are the most important elements of a professional email?
    *   (你多久写一次商务邮件？你认为专业邮件最重要的元素是什么？)

3.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   You need to `place an order` for new office supplies. Use the expressions `I'd like to place an order for...`, `We're going to need...`, and `When can we expect to receive them?` to make a phone call to the supplier.
    *   (你需要订购新的办公用品。使用 `I'd like to place an order for...`, `We're going to need...`, 和 `When can we expect to receive them?` 等表达给供应商打电话。)

4.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   Your colleague sent an email to a client using too much `text-speak`, and you know it's not `appropriate`. How would you explain to them why it's important to use formal language in business emails, referencing the phrases we learned?
    *   (你的同事给客户发了一封邮件，使用了过多的短信语言，你知道这不合适。你会如何向他们解释在商务邮件中使用正式语言的重要性，并引用我们学过的短语？)

5.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Let's role-play. One person is a customer service representative receiving an order. The other person is a client `placing an order` for a large quantity of a product. The client should ask about delivery times and `confirm` details.
    *   (我们来角色扮演。一个人是客户服务代表，接收订单。另一个人是客户，正在订购大量产品。客户应该询问交货时间并确认细节。)

6.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Imagine you received an email from a business partner that was full of `text-speak` and informal language. How would you feel about their professionalism? What impact could this have on your business relationship?
    *   (想象一下你收到一封来自商业伙伴的邮件，里面充满了短信语言和非正式语言。你对他们的专业性会有何感受？这可能对你们的业务关系产生什么影响？)
