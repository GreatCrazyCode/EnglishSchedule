
# 口语练习 (Speaking Practice) - Day 1 & 2 (Week 4)

## 话题 (Topic)
Professional phone communication and politely refusing requests.
(专业的电话沟通与礼貌地拒绝请求)

---

## 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)
*   **telephone manner**: 电话礼仪
*   **available**: 有空的，可联系的
*   **take a message**: 留言
*   **politely**: 礼貌地
*   **refuse**: 拒绝
*   **company policy**: 公司政策
*   **It wouldn't be appropriate.**: 这不合适。
*   **With regret, I'm going to have to say no.**: 遗憾的是，我不得不拒绝。

---

## 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

1.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   How do you usually answer your phone, both for personal and work calls? Are there any differences?
    *   (你通常如何接听电话，包括私人电话和工作电话？有什么区别吗？)

2.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   Is it difficult for you to say "no" to requests, especially from superiors or clients? Why or why not?
    *   (你觉得拒绝请求困难吗，特别是来自上级或客户的请求？为什么？)

3.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   A client calls and asks to speak to your colleague, who is currently `unavailable`. How would you handle the call professionally, offering to `take a message` or have your colleague call back, using the phrases we learned?
    *   (一位客户打电话要求与你的同事通话，但你的同事目前不方便。你会如何专业地处理这个电话，提供留言或让你的同事回电，使用我们学过的短语？)

4.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   Your manager asks you to attend a non-business related event with a client, but you feel `it wouldn't be appropriate` due to `company policy`. How would you `politely refuse` this request?
    *   (你的经理要求你参加一个与客户的非商务活动，但你觉得由于公司政策，这不合适。你会如何礼貌地拒绝这个请求？)

5.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Let's role-play. One person is a client asking for a favor that goes against `company policy`. The other person is an employee who needs to `politely refuse` using phrases like `I'm afraid it is against company policy...` and `It wouldn't be appropriate.`
    *   (我们来角色扮演。一个人是客户，提出一个违反公司政策的请求。另一个人是员工，需要礼貌地拒绝，使用 `I'm afraid it is against company policy...` 和 `It wouldn't be appropriate.` 等短语。)

6.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Discuss the importance of maintaining a good `telephone manner` in business. How can it impact client relationships and your professional image? What are some key elements of good telephone manner?
    *   (讨论在商务中保持良好电话礼仪的重要性。它如何影响客户关系和你的职业形象？良好电话礼仪的一些关键要素是什么？)
