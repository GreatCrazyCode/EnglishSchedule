
## 口语练习: 请求帮助与公司安全
## Speaking Practice: Asking for Help & Company Safety

### 话题 (Topic)
This practice session is about a scenario where you need to ask a senior colleague for advice on a new, challenging task. The conversation then shifts to discussing a recent company-wide reminder about health and safety procedures.
本次口语练习模拟一个场景：你需要向一位资深同事请教如何应对一项富有挑战性的新任务。接着，对话转移到讨论最近公司发布的关于健康与安全程序的提醒。

### 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)
*   grateful (感激的)
*   advice (建议)
*   spare a few minutes (抽出几分钟)
*   strategy (策略)
*   policy (政策)
*   procedure (程序)
*   raise the alarm (拉响警报)
*   fire exit (消防出口)
*   assembly point (集合点)

### 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

**Warm-up Questions (热身问题)**

1.  Do you usually ask for help when you face a difficult task, or do you prefer to figure it out on your own? Why?
    当你遇到困难的任务时，你通常会寻求帮助，还是更愿意自己解决？为什么？
2.  How important do you think health and safety procedures are in a workplace? Can you give an example?
    你认为工作场所的健康和安全程序有多重要？能举个例子吗？

**Core Questions (核心问题)**

3.  Imagine you need help from a very busy manager. How would you politely interrupt them and ask for advice using a phrase like "I know you're busy but..."?
    想象一下你需要一位非常忙碌的经理的帮助。你会如何礼貌地打断他们并使用类似 “I know you're busy but...” 的短语来请求建议？
4.  Your company just reviewed its fire safety procedures. Can you explain the main steps to a new colleague, mentioning the alarm, the fire exit, and the assembly point?
    你的公司刚刚重申了消防安全程序。你能向一位新同事解释主要的步骤吗，需要提到警报、消防出口和集合点。

**Extension Questions (扩展问题)**

5.  If a colleague asks you for advice, what is the best way to give it without sounding bossy or critical?
    如果一位同事向你寻求建议，你认为在不听起来专横或挑剔的情况下，提供建议的最佳方式是什么？
6.  Besides fire, what other potential risks or safety incidents should a company have a policy or procedure for? Why is it important to have a plan for these situations?
    除了火灾，公司还应该为哪些其他潜在风险或安全事件制定政策或程序？为这些情况制定计划为什么很重要？
