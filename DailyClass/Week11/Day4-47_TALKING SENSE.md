
# Day 4: 47. TALKING SENSE

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **rumours** /ˈruːməz/ - n. 谣言，传闻
*   **establish the facts** /ɪˈstæblɪʃ ðə fækts/ - phrase. 确定事实
*   **pass on** /pɑːs ɒn/ - phrase. 传递
*   **conference** /ˈkɒnfərəns/ - n. 会议
*   **innovation** /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃn/ - n. 创新
*   **acceptance speech** /əkˈseptəns spiːtʃ/ - n. 获奖感言

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **There are some rumours going around that are not true.** - 有一些不实的谣言在流传。
    *   *Example:* "I've called this meeting because **there are some rumours going around that are not true**, and I want to clarify the situation."
*   **Please don't listen to rumours.** - 请不要听信谣言。
    *   *Example:* "**Please don't listen to rumours**. Wait for an official announcement."
*   **I am trying to establish the facts.** - 我正在努力确定事实。
    *   *Example:* "I know you are all worried, but **I am trying to establish the facts** before I say anything."
*   **Please just carry on with your work as normal.** - 请照常继续你们的工作。
    *   *Example:* "For now, there's nothing to worry about. **Please just carry on with your work as normal**."

## 4. Exercises (练习)
1.  **Fill in the blank (填空题):**
    *   It's important to get information from a reliable source and not just \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ you hear in the office.
    *   从可靠的来源获取信息很重要，而不仅仅是听信你在办公室听到的谣言。

2.  **Situational Question (情景问答):**
    *   You are a manager. You hear a rumour that your company is going to close. This is not true. What would you say to your team to stop the panic? (你是一名经理。你听到一个谣言说你的公司要倒闭了。这不是真的。你会对你的团队说什么来制止恐慌？)
