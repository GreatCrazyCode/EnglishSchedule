
# Week 11 Review: Day 1 - Day 4

## Introduction (引言)
This week, we tackled a variety of common workplace challenges. We've learned how to handle everything from technical problems and pitching new ideas to taking on leadership responsibilities and managing misinformation within a team.
本周，我们解决了一系列常见的工作场所挑战。我们学习了如何处理从技术问题、推销新想法，到承担领导责任和管理团队内部的错误信息等各种情况。

## Topic Review (主题回顾)
*   **Day 1: Computer Crisis (电脑危机)**
    *   We learned the essential vocabulary to describe a "computer crisis" to an IT support team, including issues like a "freezing screen" and "lost documents".
    *   我们学习了向IT支持团队描述“电脑危机”的基本词汇，包括“屏幕死机”和“文件丢失”等问题。

*   **Day 2: The Elevator Pitch (电梯游说)**
    *   We practiced how to deliver a short, clear, and persuasive "elevator pitch" to sell a new business idea quickly.
    *   我们练习了如何进行简短、清晰且有说服力的“电梯游说”，以快速推销一个新的商业想法。

*   **Day 3: Acting Up (代理职务)**
    *   We focused on the language of "taking charge" and being an approachable leader when asked to "act up" for a manager.
    *   我们专注于在被要求为经理“代理职务”时，“负责”并成为一个平易近人的领导者的语言。

*   **Day 4: Managing Rumours (管理谣言)**
    *   We learned key phrases to stop the spread of office "rumours" by "establishing the facts" and communicating clearly with the team.
    *   我们学习了通过“确定事实”和与团队清晰沟通来阻止办公室“谣言”传播的关键短语。

## Core Vocabulary and Expressions (核心词汇与表达式)

### Technical & IT (技术与IT)
*   **Vocabulary:** crisis, freeze, documents, reboot, IT support
*   **Expressions:** 
    *   I am having problems with my computer.
    *   The screen keeps freezing.
    *   I have turned the computer off and on but that hasn't fixed it.

### Pitches & Leadership (推销与领导力)
*   **Vocabulary:** pitch, elevator pitch, concisely, confident, act up, take charge, approachable, issues
*   **Expressions:**
    *   We've come up with an exciting new product.
    *   I'm confident this will be a huge success.
    *   If anyone has any issues, please come and speak to me.
    *   My door is always open.

### Communication & Rumours (沟通与谣言)
*   **Vocabulary:** rumours, establish the facts, pass on
*   **Expressions:**
    *   There are some rumours going around that are not true.
    *   Please don't listen to rumours.
    *   Please just carry on with your work as normal.

## Comprehensive Exercise (综合练习)
**Scenario:** You have been asked to "act up" as manager. You immediately face two problems: a "rumour" is spreading that the company is losing money, and at the same time, the server has crashed and people are "having problems with their computers".

**Task:** Write a short announcement to your team. Address the rumour, tell them how you will handle the computer crisis, and encourage them to "carry on as normal". Use at least **4** of the key words or expressions from this week.

---
**(情景练习):** 你被要求“代理”经理。你立即面临两个问题：一个关于公司亏损的“谣言”正在传播，与此同时，服务器崩溃了，人们的“电脑都出了问题”。

**任务:** 给你的团队写一个简短的公告。回应谣言，告诉他们你将如何处理电脑危机，并鼓励他们“照常工作”。请至少使用本周学到的 **4个** 关键词或表达式。
