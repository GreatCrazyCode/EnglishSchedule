## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **crisis** /ˈkraɪsɪs/ - n. 危机
*   **freeze** /friːz/ - v. (屏幕)死机
*   **documents** /ˈdɒkjumənts/ - n. 文件
*   **IT support** /ˌaɪˈtiː səˈpɔːt/ - n. 信息技术支持
*   **application** /ˌæplɪˈkeɪʃn/ - n. 应用程序
*   **reboot** /ˌriːˈbuːt/ - v. 重启，重新启动

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **I am having problems with my computer.** - 我的电脑出问题了。
    *   *Example:* "Hello, IT support? **I am having problems with my computer** and I can't log in."
*   **The screen keeps freezing.** - 屏幕总是死机。
    *   *Example:* "I think I have a virus because **the screen keeps freezing**."
*   **I seem to have lost some documents.** - 我好像丢失了一些文件。
    *   *Example:* "After the computer crashed, **I seem to have lost some documents**. Can they be recovered?"
*   **I have turned the computer off and on but that hasn't fixed it.** - 我已经重启过电脑了，但问题没有解决。
    *   *Example:* "Yes, **I have turned the computer off and on but that hasn't fixed it**."

## 4. Exercises (练习)
