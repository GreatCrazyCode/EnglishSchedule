
# Day 3: 46. ACTING UP

## 1. Link to Material (学习资料)

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **act up** /ækt ʌp/ - phrase. 代理(某人)的职务
*   **take charge** /teɪk tʃɑːdʒ/ - phrase. 负责，掌管
*   **approachable** /əˈprəʊtʃəbl/ - adj. 平易近人的，和蔼可亲的
*   **issues** /ˈɪʃuːz/ - n. 问题，议题
*   **promotion** /prəˈməʊʃn/ - n. 晋升，提拔
*   **business as usual** /ˈbɪznəs əz ˈjuːʒuəl/ - phrase. 照常营业，一切照旧

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **If anyone has any issues about their work, please come and speak to me.** - 如果有任何人对他们的工作有任何问题，请来和我说。
    *   *Example:* "As the new team leader, I want to say that **if anyone has any issues about their work, please come and speak to me**."
*   **I would like to have a meeting to discuss our work.** - 我想开个会讨论一下我们的工作。
    *   *Example:* "To make sure we are all on the same page, **I would like to have a meeting to discuss our work** this Friday."
*   **My door is always open.** - 我的门永远敞开。(意指随时欢迎来谈)
    *   *Example:* "Remember, if you have any concerns, **my door is always open**."
