
# Day 4: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Clearing Up Confusion and Rumours (消除困惑和谣言)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **rumours** (谣言)
*   **establish the facts** (确定事实)
*   **pass them on** (传递它们)
*   **not true** (不实的)
*   **carry on as normal** (照常进行)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever heard a "rumour" that turned out to be completely untrue?
    (你曾听过一个后来证明完全是假的“谣言”吗？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Why do you think rumours spread so quickly in an office?
    (你认为为什么谣言在办公室里传播得这么快？)

3.  **Core:** As a manager, why is it important to "establish the facts" before making an announcement?
    (作为一名经理，为什么在发布公告前“确定事实”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** If you hear a negative rumour about a colleague, what should you do?
    (如果你听到关于同事的负面谣言，你应该怎么做？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine your company is going through a big change. How can the management prevent false rumours from spreading and causing panic?
    (想象你的公司正在经历一个巨大的变化。管理层如何能防止虚假谣言传播并引起恐慌？)

6.  **Extension:** What is the difference between a rumour and gossip?
    (谣言和八卦有什么区别？)
