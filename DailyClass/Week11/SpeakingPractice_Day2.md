
# Day 2: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Making an Elevator Pitch (进行电梯游说)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **elevator pitch** (电梯游说)
*   **come up with** (想出)
*   **a great addition** (一个很好的补充)
*   **increase profits** (增加利润)
*   **confident** (自信的)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** If you had to describe your job or your main hobby in 30 seconds, what would you say?
    (如果你必须在30秒内描述你的工作或主要爱好，你会说什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Why is it called an "elevator pitch"?
    (为什么它被称为“电梯游说”？)

3.  **Core:** What are the most important elements of a good elevator pitch?
    (一个好的电梯游说最重要的元素是什么？)

4.  **Core:** When you say "I'm confident this will be a huge success," what makes it sound convincing?
    (当你说“我相信这将会是一个巨大的成功”时，是什么让它听起来有说服力？)

5.  **Extension:** Think of a product you use every day. Prepare a 30-second elevator pitch to sell it to someone who has never seen it before.
    (想一个你每天都使用的产品。准备一个30秒的电梯游说，把它卖给一个从未见过它的人。)

6.  **Extension:** Besides business, in what other situations could an elevator pitch be useful?
    (除了商务，电梯游说在哪些其他情况下可能有用？)
