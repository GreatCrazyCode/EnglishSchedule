
# Day 3: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Being in Charge (负责)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **act up** (代理职务)
*   **take charge** (负责)
*   **approachable** (平易近人的)
*   **have a meeting** (开会)
*   **my door is always open** (我的门永远敞开)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever been "in charge" of a project or a team? Did you enjoy it?
    (你曾“负责”过一个项目或一个团队吗？你喜欢吗？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What are the most important qualities of a good leader?
    (一个好领导最重要的品质是什么？)

3.  **Core:** What does it mean when a boss says, "My door is always open"?
    (当老板说“我的门永远敞开”时，这是什么意思？)

4.  **Core:** If you were in charge, how would you make sure every team member felt their "issues" were heard?
    (如果你是负责人，你会如何确保每个团队成员都觉得他们的“问题”被听到了？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you have to "act up" for your boss for a day. What would be your biggest challenge?
    (想象你必须为你的老板“代理”一天。你最大的挑战会是什么？)

6.  **Extension:** What is the difference between being a "bossy" leader and an "approachable" leader?
    (一个“专横”的领导和一个“平易近人”的领导有什么区别？)
