
# Day 1: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Dealing with IT Support (与IT支持打交道)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **computer problems** (电脑问题)
*   **screen freezing** (屏幕死机)
*   **lost documents** (丢失文件)
*   **reboot** (重启)
*   **IT support** (IT支持)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Are you good with computers? What do you usually do when you have a computer problem?
    (你擅长电脑吗？当你有电脑问题时，你通常会怎么做？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What is the most frustrating computer problem you have ever had?
    (你遇到过的最令人沮丧的电脑问题是什么？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it a good idea to tell IT support that you have already tried to "reboot" the computer?
    (为什么告诉IT支持你已经尝试过“重启”电脑是个好主意？)

4.  **Core:** If you "lost some documents," what information would be helpful for IT support to know?
    (如果你“丢失了一些文件”，哪些信息对IT支持会有帮助？)

5.  **Extension:** Besides work documents, what other important information on your computer should you back up regularly?
    (除了工作文件，你电脑上还有哪些其他重要信息应该定期备份？)

6.  **Extension:** Some people get very angry when they have computer problems. What is the best way to stay calm and communicate effectively with IT support?
    (有些人在遇到电脑问题时会非常生气。保持冷静并与IT支持有效沟通的最佳方式是什么？)
