
# Week 7 Review: Day 1 - Day 4

本周，我们专注于专业的沟通和商业策略，从保持对话的得体性到谈判交易和制定市场策略。我们学习了如何处理困难的对话，寻找新客户，以及战略性地思考业务增长。

## Core Vocabulary and Expressions (核心词汇与表达式)

### Professionalism & Communication (专业与沟通)
*   **Vocabulary:** hospitality, appropriate, professional, interrupt
*   **Expressions:** 
    *   It's not appropriate to talk like that.
    *   Could we change the subject, please?
    *   Can we keep our conversation professional, please?

### Sales & Negotiation (销售与谈判)
*   **Vocabulary:** cold calling, prospective client, track down, potential, negotiate, discount, stock
*   **Expressions:**
    *   Can you spare a few minutes of your time?
    *   I'd like to tell you about...
    *   What kind of price are you willing to pay?
    *   I don't think we can go that low.
    *   I'll meet you halfway.

### Strategy & Business (策略与商务)
*   **Vocabulary:** strategy, rival, dominate, relationship, contract
*   **Expressions:**
    *   We need to touch base about...
    *   Have they signed on the dotted line yet?
    *   We've got to compete on price.
    *   We need to corner the market.

## Comprehensive Exercise (综合练习)
**Scenario:** You are having a meeting with a new, "potential" client. You need to "negotiate" a price for a large order. Your goal is to get them to "sign on the dotted line" without offering too large of a "discount".

**Task:** Write a short dialogue of the negotiation. Use at least **4** of the key words or expressions from this week.

---
**(情景对话):** 你正在与一个新的“潜在”客户开会。你需要就一份大订单进行价格“谈判”。你的目标是让他们在不提供太大“折扣”的情况下“在合同上签字”。

**任务:** 写一段简短的谈判对话。请至少使用本周学到的 **4个** 核心词汇或表达式。
