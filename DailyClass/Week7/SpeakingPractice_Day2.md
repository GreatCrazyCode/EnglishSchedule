
# Day 2: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Making a Sales Pitch (进行销售宣传)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **cold calling** (陌生电话)
*   **prospective client** (潜在客户)
*   **track down** (找到)
*   **spare a few minutes** (抽出几分钟)
*   **tell you about...** (告诉你关于...)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever received a "cold call"? How did you react?
    (你接到过“陌生推销电话”吗？你作何反应？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What makes a product presentation interesting and effective?
    (什么能让一个产品介绍变得有趣和有效？)

3.  **Core:** If you had to make a cold call, how would you start the conversation to get the person's attention?
    (如果你必须打一个陌生推销电话，你会如何开始对话以吸引对方的注意？)

4.  **Core:** When you say, "Can you spare a few minutes of your time?", what do you think is the best way to get a "yes"?
    (当你说“你能抽出几分钟时间吗？”时，你认为获得肯定回答的最佳方式是什么？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you are trying to sell a new type of eco-friendly coffee cup to an office manager. What would you say in your pitch?
    (想象一下，你正在尝试向一位办公室经理推销一种新型的环保咖啡杯。在你的宣传中你会说些什么？)

6.  **Extension:** What are the biggest challenges of cold calling, and how can you overcome them?
    (陌生电话推销最大的挑战是什么，你如何克服它们？)
