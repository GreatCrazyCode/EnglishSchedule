## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **negotiate** /nəˈɡəʊʃieɪt/ - v. 谈判，协商
*   **place an order** /pleɪs ən ˈɔːdə(r)/ - phrase. 下订单
*   **discount** /ˈdɪskaʊnt/ - n. 折扣
*   **stock** /stɒk/ - n. 库存，存货
*   **impressed** /ɪmˈprest/ - adj. 印象深刻的
*   **contract** /ˈkɒntrækt/ - n. 合同

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **What kind of price are you willing to pay?** - 您愿意支付什么样的价格？
    *   *Example:* "Before we continue, let's be clear: **what kind of price are you willing to pay?**"
*   **I don't think we can go that low.** - 我认为我们不能降到那么低。
    *   *Example:* "You're asking for a 50% discount? **I don't think we can go that low.**"
*   **If you buy more stock, I can offer you a bigger discount.** - 如果您购买更多库存，我可以给您更大的折扣。
    *   *Example:* "**If you buy more stock**, for example 1,000 units instead of 500, **I can offer you a bigger discount**."
*   **I'll meet you halfway.** - 我各让一半。
    *   *Example:* "You want a 20% discount, and I'm offering 10%. Let's agree to 15% - **I'll meet you halfway**."
