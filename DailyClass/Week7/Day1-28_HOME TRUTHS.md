
# Day 1: 28. HOME TRUTHS

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **hospitality** /ˌhɒspɪˈtæləti/ - n. 好客，殷勤
*   **courtesy** /ˈkɜːtəsi/ - n. 礼貌，谦恭
*   **appropriate** /əˈprəʊpriət/ - adj. 适当的，恰当的
*   **professional** /prəˈfeʃənl/ - adj. 专业的，职业的
*   **strategy** /ˈstrætədʒi/ - n. 策略，战略
*   **interrupt** /ˌɪntəˈrʌpt/ - v. 打断，插嘴

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **It's not appropriate to talk like that.** - 那样说话是不恰当的。
    *   *Example:* "Please don't discuss personal salaries at work; **it's not appropriate to talk like that**."
*   **Could we change the subject, please?** - 我们可以换个话题吗？
    *   *Example:* "This discussion is getting too heated. **Could we change the subject, please?**"
*   **Can we keep our conversation professional, please?** - 我们的谈话能保持专业吗？
    *   *Example:* "I understand you're upset, but **can we keep our conversation professional, please?**"
