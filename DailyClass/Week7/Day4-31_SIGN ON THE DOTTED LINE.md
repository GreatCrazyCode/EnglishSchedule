
# Day 4: 31. SIGN ON THE DOTTED LINE

## 1. Link to Material (学习资料)

## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **touch base** /tʌtʃ beɪs/ - phrase. (与...）取得联系，碰头
*   **strategy** /ˈstrætədʒi/ - n. 策略，战略
*   **sign on the dotted line** /saɪn ɒn ðə ˈdɒtɪd laɪn/ - phrase. 在虚线上签名（表示同意或接受）
*   **rival** /ˈraɪvl/ - n. 对手，竞争者
*   **dominate** /ˈdɒmɪneɪt/ - v. 主导，支配
*   **relationship** /rɪˈleɪʃnʃɪp/ - n. 关系

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **We need to touch base about...** - 我们需要就...碰个头。
    *   *Example:* "**We need to touch base about** the new marketing plan before the meeting."
*   **Have they signed on the dotted line yet?** - 他们在合同上签字了吗？
    *   *Example:* "The negotiation was successful, but **have they signed on the dotted line yet?**"
*   **We've got to compete on price.** - 我们必须在价格上竞争。
    *   *Example:* "Our competitor's product is very similar, so **we've got to compete on price**."
*   **We need to corner the market.** - 我们需要垄断市场。
    *   *Example:* "With this new technology, **we need to corner the market** before our rivals catch up."
