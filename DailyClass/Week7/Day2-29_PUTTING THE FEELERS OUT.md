## 2. Useful Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **cold calling** /kəʊld ˈkɔːlɪŋ/ - n. 冷不防地打电话（或上门）进行推销
*   **prospective** /prəˈspektɪv/ - adj. 未来的，潜在的
*   **client** /ˈklaɪənt/ - n. 客户，委托人
*   **track down** /træk daʊn/ - phrase. 追踪，找到
*   **potential** /pəˈtenʃl/ - adj. 潜在的，可能的
*   **brief** /briːf/ - adj. 简短的
*   **manufacturer** /ˌmænjuˈfæktʃərə(r)/ - n. 制造商

## 3. Key Expressions (关键表达式)
*   **Can you spare a few minutes of your time?** - 你能抽出几分钟时间吗？
    *   *Example:* "Hello, my name is John. **Can you spare a few minutes of your time** to talk about your energy supplier?"
*   **I’d like to tell you about...** - 我想告诉你关于...
    *   *Example:* "**I'd like to tell you about** our new and improved software."
*   **How much... do you buy?** - 你购买多少...？
    *   *Example:* "**How much** coffee **do you buy** for your office each month?"
*   **Thank you for your time.** - 感谢您抽出时间。
    *   *Example:* "I understand you're busy, so **thank you for your time**."

## 4. Exercises (练习)
1.  **Fill in the blank (填空题):**

2.  **Situational Question (情景问答):**
