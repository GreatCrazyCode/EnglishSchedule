
# Day 3: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Negotiating a Deal (谈判交易)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **negotiate** (谈判)
*   **discount** (折扣)
*   **go that low** (降那么低)
*   **buy more stock** (购买更多库存)
*   **meet you halfway** (各让一半)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Do you enjoy negotiating, or do you find it stressful? Why?
    (你喜欢谈判吗，还是觉得有压力？为什么？)

2.  **Warm-up:** What is the most important skill for a successful negotiation?
    (成功谈判最重要的技巧是什么？)

3.  **Core:** When a client says your price is too high, what is a polite way to respond?
    (当客户说你的价格太高时，礼貌的回应方式是什么？)

4.  **Core:** What does it mean to "meet someone halfway" in a negotiation? Can you give an example?
    (在谈判中“各让一半”是什么意思？你能举个例子吗？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine you are selling your old car. A buyer offers you a price that is too low. How would you negotiate for a better price?
    (想象一下你正在卖你的旧车。一个买家出价太低。你会如何谈判以获得更好的价格？)

6.  **Extension:** Besides price, what other things can be negotiated in a business deal?
    (除了价格，商务交易中还有哪些其他可以谈判的东西？)
