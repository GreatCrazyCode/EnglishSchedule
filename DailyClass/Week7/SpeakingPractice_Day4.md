
# Day 4: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Developing a Business Strategy (制定商业策略)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **touch base** (碰头)
*   **strategy** (策略)
*   **sign on the dotted line** (签字)
*   **compete on price** (在价格上竞争)
*   **corner the market** (垄断市场)
*   **build good relationships** (建立良好关系)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** What makes a company successful in your opinion?
    (你认为什么能让一家公司成功？)

2.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever been part of planning a project or a strategy?
    (你曾参与过项目或策略的规划吗？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it important to "touch base" with your team regularly when working on a project?
    (在项目上工作时，为什么定期与团队“碰头”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** What are the advantages and disadvantages of trying to "compete on price"?
    (试图“在价格上竞争”的优缺点是什么？)

5.  **Extension:** If you wanted to "corner the market" with a new product, what are three key things you would do?
    (如果你想用一个新产品“垄断市场”，你会做的三件关键事情是什么？)

6.  **Extension:** Besides price, what are other ways to compete with a "rival" company?
    (除了价格，还有哪些其他方式可以与“竞争对手”公司竞争？)
