
# Day 1: Speaking Practice (口语练习)

## Topic (话题)
Keeping Conversations Professional (保持对话的专业性)

## Key Vocabulary (核心词汇)
*   **appropriate** (适当的)
*   **professional** (专业的)
*   **change the subject** (改变话题)
*   **interrupt** (打断)
*   **hospitality** (好客)

## Practice Questions (练习问题)
1.  **Warm-up:** Have you ever been in a conversation that made you feel uncomfortable? What was it about?
    (你是否曾参与过让你感到不舒服的对话？是关于什么的？)

2.  **Warm-up:** In your culture, what topics are considered not "appropriate" to discuss at work?
    (在你的文化中，哪些话题被认为不适合在工作中讨论？)

3.  **Core:** Why is it important to "keep our conversation professional" with colleagues and bosses?
    (为什么与同事和老板“保持对话的专业性”很重要？)

4.  **Core:** If a colleague starts gossiping about another team member, how can you "change the subject" politely?
    (如果一个同事开始八卦另一个团队成员，你如何能礼貌地“改变话题”？)

5.  **Extension:** Imagine your boss is making jokes that you find inappropriate. It's a difficult situation. What would you do or say?
    (想象一下你的老板在开一些你认为不恰当的玩笑。这是一个困难的处境。你会怎么做或说什么？)

6.  **Extension:** Is it ever okay to be personal with colleagues? Where do you draw the line between being friendly and being unprofessional?
    (与同事变得个人化可以吗？你在友好和不专业之间如何划清界限？)
