
# TOPIC AND QUESTIONS FOR TODAY'S SESSION

## Topic
Handling work-related problems, apologising, and giving praise.

## Key Vocabulary and Expressions to Practice
*   **Vocabulary**: apologise, unacceptable, inconvenient, sort out, praise, responsible for, professional, impressed
*   **Expressions for Apologising**:
    *   I'm really sorry to hear that.
    *   It must have been very inconvenient for you.
    *   You have my word that we will sort this out.
    *   to make up for the inconvenience.
*   **Expressions for Giving Praise**:
    *   Well done.
    *   You handled it well.
    *   Great job.
    *   I'm really impressed.

## Practice Questions

**Warm-up (1-2 questions):**
1.  Tell me about a time you received really good customer service. What made it good?
2.  Have you ever received praise for your work? How did it make you feel?

**Core Topic (2-3 questions):**
3.  Let's role-play. I am an angry customer in your restaurant. My order was wrong and I've been waiting for 30 minutes. What would you say to me? Please use at least two expressions for apologising and offer a solution.
4.  Now, let's switch roles. I am your employee, Anna. I just successfully handled a very difficult client. You are my manager. How would you praise my work? Please use at least three different expressions.
5.  Let's try a manager scenario. I'll play two of your employees, Tom and Anna. You (as the manager) need to talk to them. First, criticise Tom for his poor performance, then praise Anna for her excellent work. Be ready to explain your reasons.

**Extension (1-2 questions):**
6.  In your opinion, what is more important for a manager: to be good at criticising mistakes or to be good at giving praise? Why?
7.  How do you motivate yourself after making a mistake at work? What's a good way to recover?
