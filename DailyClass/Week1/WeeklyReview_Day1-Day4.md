
# Weekly Review: Day 1 - Day 4

## 1. 引言 (Introduction)

本周我们专注于提升职场沟通的关键技能。我们学习了如何礼貌地请求帮助，如何主动提供援助，如何专业地道歉，以及如何恰当地给予表扬。这些都是建立良好同事关系和高效完成工作的重要技巧。

This week, we focused on enhancing key communication skills in the workplace. We learned how to make polite requests, offer help proactively, apologise professionally, and give praise appropriately. These are all crucial skills for building good relationships with colleagues and working effectively.

## 2. 主题回顾 (Topic Review)

*   **Day 1: THE QUEEN OF SHEBA** - 学习了如何使用 `Could you`, `Would you`, `Would you mind` 等句式进行礼貌的求助。
*   **Day 2: DOUBLE-BOOKED** - 学习了如何使用 `Is there anything I can do?`, `Let me help` 等句式主动向同事提供帮助。
*   **Day 3: MR LIME'S GRAPEFRUITS** - 学习了在工作失误后，如何通过 `I'm really sorry`, `You have my word` 等表达来真诚地道歉和解决问题。
*   **Day 4: ROOM 301** - 学习了如何使用 `Well done`, `Great job`, `I'm really impressed` 等方式来表扬和激励同事。

## 3. 核心词汇和短语 (Core Vocabulary and Expressions)

### 动词 / 动词短语 (Verbs / Phrasal Verbs)
*   apologise (道歉)
*   cope with (处理；应付)
*   sort out (解决)
*   step in (介入)
*   deserve (值得)

### 名词 (Nouns)
*   colleague (同事)
*   chaos (混乱)
*   disaster (灾难)
*   complaint (投诉)
*   proposal (提议)
*   customer service (客户服务)

### 形容词 (Adjectives)
*   urgent (紧急的)
*   bossy (爱指挥人的)
*   inconvenient (不方便的)
*   unacceptable (不能接受的)
*   professional (专业的)
*   tense (紧张的)

### 关键表达式 (Key Expressions)

**请求与帮助 (Requesting & Offering Help):**
*   I was wondering if you could...
*   Would you be able to...?
*   Could you possibly...?
*   Is there anything I can do?
*   Can I give you a hand?

**道歉与承诺 (Apologising & Promising):**
*   I'm really sorry to hear that.
*   It must have been very inconvenient for you.
*   You have my word that we will sort this out.

**给予表扬 (Giving Praise):**
*   Well done.
*   You handled it well.
*   I'm really impressed.

## 4. 综合练习 (Comprehensive Exercise)

**情景写作：一封工作邮件 (Scenario Writing: A Work Email)**

*   **背景**: 你的同事 Alex 负责的一个项目出现了紧急问题 (an **urgent** issue)，导致客户非常不满并进行了投诉 (a **complaint**)。你主动介入 (**stepped in**)，通过与客户沟通，最终成功解决了问题。现在，请你给你的经理写一封邮件，汇报整个事件。

*   **要求**: 
    1.  简单描述问题的背景。
    2.  说明你是如何处理这个问题的。
    3.  在邮件中，你需要对 Alex 的工作进行评价。你可以选择赞扬他做得好的地方，或者客观描述情况。
    4.  邮件中必须使用至少 **4个** 本周学习的核心词汇或关键表达式。

*   **请在下方写出你的邮件:**

---

Subject: Update on the issue with Client X

Hi [Manager's Name],



Best regards,

[Your Name]

---
