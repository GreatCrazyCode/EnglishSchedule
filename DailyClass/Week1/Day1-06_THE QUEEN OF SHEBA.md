## 3. Key Expressions (3-5个)
*   **I was wondering if you could do something for me...**
    *   中文解释: 我想知道你是否能帮我做件事…… (一种非常礼貌的请求方式)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "I was wondering if you could help me with this report."
        *   "I was wondering if you could cover my shift tomorrow."
*   **Would you be able to...?**
    *   中文解释: 你能……吗？ (比 "Can you...?" 更礼貌)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Would you be able to join the meeting at 3 PM?"
        *   "Would you be able to send me the file when you have a moment?"
*   **Could you possibly...?**
    *   中文解释: 你是否有可能……？ (非常客气的请求，强调对方可能会感到为难)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Could you possibly stay a little later today to finish this?"
        *   "Could you possibly change the appointment for me?"
*   **Would you mind...?**
    *   中文解释: 你介意……吗？ (后跟动词的-ing形式)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Would you mind opening the window? It's a bit hot in here."
        *   "Would you mind not talking so loudly?"

## 4. Exercises (2个)
*   **练习1: 填空题 (Fill in the blanks)**
    1.  If you need help, you should ask a __________. (如果你需要帮助，你应该去问同事。)
    2.  "__________ you mind helping me with this heavy box?" (你介意帮我搬一下这个很重的箱子吗？)
    3.  I need the report by noon, it's quite __________. (我中午前需要这份报告，它非常紧急。)
    4.  "__________ you possibly lend me your pen for a second?" (你是否有可能把你的笔借我用一下？)

*   **练习2: 情景问答 (Situational Question)**
    *   You need your manager to approve your vacation request, but you know she is very busy today. How would you ask her politely using one of the key expressions from this lesson?
    *   (你需要你的经理批准你的休假申请，但你知道她今天非常忙。你会如何用今天学到的一个关键表达式来礼貌地询问她？)
