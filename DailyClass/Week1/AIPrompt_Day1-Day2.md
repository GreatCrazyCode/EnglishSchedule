
# TOPIC AND QUESTIONS FOR TODAY'S SESSION

## Topic
Asking for and offering help in a busy office environment.

## Key Vocabulary and Expressions to Practice
*   **Vocabulary**: colleague, urgent, cope with, chaos, disaster, timetable
*   **Expressions for Polite Requests**:
    *   I was wondering if you could...
    *   Would you be able to...?
    *   Could you possibly...?
    *   Would you mind...?
*   **Expressions for Offering Help**:
    *   Is there anything I can do?
    *   Let me help.
    *   Can I give you a hand?
    *   Give me a shout if you need anything.

## Practice Questions

**Warm-up (1-2 questions):**
1.  How has your week been so far? Have you been busy at work/study?
2.  Have you ever had to ask for help with something urgent? What happened?

**Core Topic (2-3 questions):**
3.  Imagine your computer crashes while you are working on a very urgent report. I'll be your colleague, Tom, who looks very busy. How would you ask me for help? Please use at least two polite request expressions.
4.  Now, let's switch roles. You see me (your colleague, Denise) looking very stressed and my desk is a mess. You want to offer help. What would you say? Try to use at least two different phrases for offering help.
5.  Let's try a more complex scenario. You've double-booked a meeting room and two important clients have arrived at the same time. It's a chaotic situation. I'm your colleague. Start by asking me for some help, and then respond when I offer to help you with one of the clients.

**Extension (1-2 questions):**
6.  In your culture, is it more common to ask for help directly or indirectly? How does this compare to the polite English phrases we've practiced?
7.  If someone asks you for help but you are too busy, how would you politely refuse? What could you say?
