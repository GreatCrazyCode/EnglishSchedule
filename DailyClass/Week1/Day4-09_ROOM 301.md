## 2. Useful Vocabulary (5-8个)
*   **praise** /preɪz/ - n. & v. 赞扬；表扬
*   **deserve** /dɪˈzɜːv/ - v. 值得；应得
*   **tense** /tens/ - adj. 紧张的
*   **complaint** /kəmˈpleɪnt/ - n. 投诉；抱怨
*   **responsible for** /rɪˈspɒnsəbl fɔː(r)/ - adj. ph. 对……负责
*   **salesman** /ˈseɪlzmən/ - n. 推销员；销售员
*   **proposal** /prəˈpəʊzl/ - n. 提议；建议
*   **professional** /prəˈfeʃənl/ - adj. 专业的

## 3. Key Expressions (3-5个)
*   **Well done.**
    *   中文解释: 做得好。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "You finished the project ahead of schedule. Well done!"
        *   "Well done on passing your exam!"
*   **You handled it well.**
    *   中文解释: 你处理得很好。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "That was a difficult customer, but you handled it well."
        *   "The presentation had some technical issues, but you handled it well."
*   **Great job.**
    *   中文解释: 干得漂亮。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Great job on the report, it was very thorough."
        *   "Everyone loved your idea. Great job!"
*   **I'm really impressed.**
    *   中文解释: 我印象非常深刻。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "I saw your presentation yesterday. I'm really impressed."
        *   "You learned that so quickly! I'm really impressed."

## 4. Exercises (2个)
*   **练习1: 填空题 (Fill in the blanks)**
    1.  The atmosphere in the room was very __________ before the big announcement. (在重大宣布之前，房间里的气氛非常紧张。)
    2.  He is __________ for the marketing department. (他负责市场部。)
    3.  Your work on this project has been excellent. I'm really __________. (你在这个项目上的工作非常出色，给我留下了深刻的印象。)
    4.  That was a tough negotiation, but you __________ it well. (那是一场艰难的谈判，但你处理得很好。)

*   **练习2: 情景问答 (Situational Question)**
    *   Your team member has just given a fantastic presentation to a new client. You want to praise their work. What would you say? Use at least two expressions from this lesson.
    *   (你的团队成员刚刚向一位新客户做了一场精彩的演讲。你想要表扬他们的工作。你会说什么？请至少使用本课中的两个表达式。)
