## 3. Key Expressions (3-5个)
*   **I'm really sorry to hear that.**
    *   中文解释: 听到这个消息我真的很难过/很抱歉。(用于对坏消息表示同情和歉意)
    *   应用场景:
        *   (A friend tells you they lost their job) "I'm really sorry to hear that. I hope you find something new soon."
        *   (A customer complains about a faulty product) "I'm really sorry to hear that. Let's see how we can fix it."
*   **That's totally unacceptable.**
    *   中文解释: 那是完全不能接受的。(表示强烈的反对或不赞同)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "The way he spoke to the client was totally unacceptable."
        *   "Receiving the wrong order twice is totally unacceptable."
*   **It must have been very inconvenient for you.**
    *   中文解释: 那一定给您带来了很多不便。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "The flight was cancelled? It must have been very inconvenient for you."
        *   "I understand the road closure delayed you. It must have been very inconvenient for you."
*   **You have my word that we will sort this out.**
    *   中文解释: 我向您保证，我们会解决这个问题。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Please don't worry. You have my word that we will sort this out by the end of the day."
        *   "I know it's a mess, but you have my word that we will sort this out."
*   **to make up for the inconvenience.**
    *   中文解释: 为了弥补所造成的不便。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Please accept this voucher to make up for the inconvenience."
        *   "We would like to offer you a free upgrade to make up for the inconvenience."

## 4. Exercises (2个)
*   **练习1: 填空题 (Fill in the blanks)**
    1.  The hotel was dirty and the service was poor. It was __________ __________! (这家酒店又脏服务又差，简直完全不能接受！)
    2.  I promise we will __________ this problem out for you. (我保证我们会为您解决这个问题。)
    3.  Your delivery was late? I'm so sorry, it must have been very __________ for you. (您的快递迟到了？非常抱歉，那一定给您带来了很多不便。)
    4.  As a gesture of goodwill, we'd like to offer you a discount to __________ for the inconvenience. (为了表示我们的诚意，我们想给您一个折扣以弥补给您带来的不便。)

*   **练习2: 情景问答 (Situational Question)**
    *   Imagine you are a customer service representative. A customer calls to complain that they received the wrong product. How would you apologise and promise to solve the problem, using at least two expressions from this lesson?
    *   (想象你是一名客服代表。一位顾客打电话来投诉他们收到了错误的商品。你会如何道歉并承诺解决问题，至少使用本课中的两个表达式？)
