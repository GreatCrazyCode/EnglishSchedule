## 3. Key Expressions (3-5个)
*   **Is there anything I can do?**
    *   中文解释: 有什么我能做的吗？ (一种主动提供帮助的礼貌方式)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "You look stressed. Is there anything I can do to help?"
        *   "I heard about the project deadline. Is there anything I can do?"
*   **Let me help.**
    *   中文解释: 让我来帮忙。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "That looks heavy. Let me help you with it."
        *   "You seem to be struggling with that software. Let me help."
*   **Can I give you a hand?**
    *   中文解释: 我能帮你一下吗？ (一种比较非正式的提供帮助的方式)
    *   应用场景:
        *   "Can I give you a hand with those boxes?"
        *   "I'm free now. Can I give you a hand with the preparations?"
*   **Give me a shout if you need anything.**
    *   中文解释: 如果你需要任何东西，随时叫我。
    *   应用场景:
        *   "I'll be in my office. Give me a shout if you need anything."
        *   "I'm heading out for lunch, but give me a shout if anything urgent comes up."

## 4. Exercises (2个)
*   **练习1: 填空题 (Fill in the blanks)**
    1.  The office was in complete __________ after the system crash. (系统崩溃后，办公室陷入一片混乱。)
    2.  "You look busy. Can I __________ you a hand?" (你看起来很忙，我能帮你一下吗？)
    3.  It's a difficult situation, but I'm sure we can __________ with it. (情况很困难，但我相信我们能应付。)
    4.  "I'm going to the kitchen. Give me a __________ if you need a coffee." (我要去厨房，如果你需要咖啡就叫我。)
