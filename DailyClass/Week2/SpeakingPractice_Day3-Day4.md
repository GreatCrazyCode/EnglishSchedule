
# 口语练习 (Speaking Practice) - Day 3 & Day 4

这份练习旨在将前两天所学的“讨论工作时长”与“构建演讲开场白”相结合。请思考并大声说出你的回答，这会帮助你更好地掌握这些表达方式。

This exercise is designed to combine what you've learned in the past two days: "discussing work hours" and "structuring a presentation opening." Think about and say your answers out loud to help you master these expressions.

---

### 话题 (Topic)

**情景: 准备重要的工作汇报**
**Scenario: Preparing for an Important Work Presentation**

你为了一个重要的工作汇报，最近一直在加班。你的朋友注意到了你的疲惫，并与你讨论了工作与生活的平衡。同时，你也需要构思一下你汇报的开场白。
You've been working overtime recently for an important presentation. Your friend has noticed how tired you are and discusses work-life balance with you. At the same time, you need to structure the opening of your presentation.

### 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)

*   **讨论工作时长 (Discussing Work Hours):**
    *   `overtime` (加班)
    *   `burning the candle at both ends` (过度劳累)
    *   `burn out` (累垮)
    *   `putting in the hours` (投入大量时间)
*   **演讲开场 (Presentation Opening):**
    *   `Today I'm going to...` (今天我将要...)
    *   `I'll start by...` (我将从...开始)
    *   `Then I'm going to move on to...` (接着，我将继续...)
    *   `And finally...` (最后...)

### 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

1.  **热身问题 (Warm-up):**
    *   Have you ever given a presentation? How did you feel before it?
    *   你做过演讲吗？演讲之前你感觉如何？

2.  **核心问题 (Core Questions):**
    *   A friend sees that you look exhausted and says, "You look so tired!" How would you explain your situation using the phrase `"burning the candle at both ends"`?
    *   一位朋友看到你看起来筋疲力尽，说：“你看起来太累了！” 你会如何用 `"burning the candle at both ends"` 这个短语来解释你的情况？

3.  **核心问题 (Core Questions):**
    *   Your friend is worried you might `"burn out"`. What advice would you give to a friend in this situation to help them find a better work-life balance?
    *   你的朋友担心你可能会 `"burn out"`（累垮）。在这种情况下，你会给朋友什么建议来帮助他们找到更好的工作与生活平衡？

4.  **核心问题 (Core Questions):**
    *   You need to give a short presentation about a movie you recently watched. Use the structure `"Today I'm going to..."`, `"I'll start by..."`, `"Then I'm going to move on to..."`, and `"And finally..."` to create your opening.
    *   你需要就你最近看的一部电影做一个简短的演讲。请使用 `"Today I'm going to..."`, `"I'll start by..."`, `"Then I'm going to move on to..."` 和 `"And finally..."` 这个结构来创建你的开场白。

5.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question):**
    *   Do you think `"putting in the hours"` always leads to a better result? Or is working smart more important than working hard? What's your opinion?
    *   你认为 `"putting in the hours"`（投入大量时间）总能带来更好的结果吗？还是说，聪明地工作比努力工作更重要？你的看法是什么？
