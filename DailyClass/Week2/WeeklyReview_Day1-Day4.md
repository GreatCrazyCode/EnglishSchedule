
# Week 2 复习总结 (Weekly Review)

## 1. 引言 (Introduction)

本周我们聚焦于“职场有效沟通”的几个关键方面：从如何清晰地核对信息、礼貌地提出不同意见，到讨论工作负荷和构建一个结构清晰的演讲开场。这些都是提升专业沟通能力的重要技能。

This week, we focused on several key aspects of "Effective Communication in the Workplace": from how to check information clearly and politely disagree, to discussing workload and structuring a clear presentation opening. These are all important skills for enhancing professional communication.

## 2. 主题回顾 (Topic Recap)

*   **Day 1: The Warehouse (信息核对)**
    *   核心内容: 学习了在工作中如何使用 `I just want to make sure that...` 和 `Could you possibly clarify...` 等句式来礼貌、清晰地核对信息。

*   **Day 2: The Brainstorm (表达异议)**
    *   核心内容: 练习了如何通过 `I see your point, but...` 等表达方式，在表示尊重的同时，建设性地提出不同观点。

*   **Day 3: Overtime (讨论工作负荷)**
    *   核心内容: 学习了描述“加班”和“过度劳累”的常用表达，如 `burning the candle at both ends` 和 `burn out`。

*   **Day 4: The Big Day (演讲开场)**
    *   核心内容: 掌握了构建专业演讲开场白的四个关键步骤，让你的演讲从一开始就结构分明。

## 3. 核心词汇和短语 (Core Vocabulary and Phrases)

### 动词/动词短语 (Verbs/Phrasal Verbs)
*   `clarify` (澄清)
*   `disagree` (不同意)
*   `interrupt` (打断)
*   `pay off` (得到回报)
*   `burn out` (累垮)

### 名词 (Nouns)
*   `warehouse` (仓库)
*   `delivery` (送货)
*   `viewpoint` (观点)
*   `discount` (折扣)
*   `overtime` (加班)
*   `presentation` (演讲)
*   `impression` (印象)

### 关键表达式 (Key Expressions)
*   **信息确认 (Checking Information):**
    *   `I just want to make sure that...`
    *   `Could you possibly clarify...`
    *   `Just to be absolutely clear...`
*   **表达异议 (Disagreeing Politely):**
    *   `I'm not so sure about that...`
    *   `I see your point, but...`
*   **描述劳累 (Describing Hard Work):**
    *   `burning the candle at both ends`
    *   `putting in the hours`
*   **演讲结构 (Structuring a Presentation):**
    *   `Today I'm going to...`
    *   `I'll start by...`
    *   `Then I'm going to move on to...`
    *   `And finally...`
