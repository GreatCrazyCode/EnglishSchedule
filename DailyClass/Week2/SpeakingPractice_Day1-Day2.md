
# 口语练习 (Speaking Practice) - Day 1 & Day 2

这份练习旨在将前两天所学的“信息核对”与“礼貌地表达异议”相结合，应用于一个真实的职场情景中。请准备好你的回答，可以大声说出来，就像在进行真实的对话一样。

This exercise is designed to combine what you've learned in the past two days: "checking information" and "disagreeing politely" in a realistic workplace scenario. Prepare your answers and try to say them out loud as if you were in a real conversation.

---

### 话题 (Topic)

**情景: 策划一次促销活动**
**Scenario: Planning a Sales Promotion**

你正在和同事Tom一起为新产品策划一场促销活动。Tom提出了一些你不太同意的想法，同时，你还需要向他核实一些活动细节。
You are planning a sales promotion for a new product with your colleague, Tom. Tom has suggested some ideas you don't quite agree with, and you also need to check some details about the event with him.

### 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)

*   **核实信息 (Checking Information):**
    *   `make sure` (确认)
    *   `clarify` (澄清)
    *   `Just to be absolutely clear...` (为了绝对清楚...)
*   **表达异议 (Disagreeing Politely):**
    *   `I'm not so sure about that...` (关于那点我不太确定...)
    *   `I see your point, but...` (我明白你的意思，但是...)
*   **其他 (Others):**
    *   `brainstorm` (头脑风暴)
    *   `discount` (折扣)
    *   `warehouse` (仓库)

### 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

1.  **热身问题 (Warm-up):**
    *   Do you prefer working alone or brainstorming with a team? Why?
    *   你更喜欢独立工作还是和团队一起进行头脑风暴？为什么？

2.  **核心问题 (Core Questions):**
    *   Your colleague Tom says, "I've already told the warehouse to prepare 500 units for the promotion." You haven't confirmed this number yet. How would you use a phrase like `"I just want to make sure..."` to check this information with him?
    *   你的同事Tom说：“我已经通知仓库为这次促销活动准备500个单位的产品了。” 你尚未确认这个数字。你会如何使用像 `"I just want to make sure..."` 这样的句式来与他核实这个信息？

3.  **核心问题 (Core Questions):**
    *   Tom suggests, "Let's offer a 70% discount! It will attract a lot of customers." You think this discount is too high and might hurt profits. How would you use `"I see your point, but..."` to express your disagreement and suggest a different idea?
    *   Tom建议：“我们打三折吧！这样会吸引很多顾客。” 你觉得这个折扣太高，可能会影响利润。你会如何使用 `"I see your point, but..."` 来表达你的不同意见，并提出一个不同的想法？

4.  **核心问题 (Core Questions):**
    *   Tom says, "We should announce the promotion tomorrow." You think it's too soon. How would you use `"I'm not so sure about that..."` to question his timing?
    *   Tom说：“我们应该明天就宣布促销活动。” 你觉得这太快了。你会如何使用 `"I'm not so sure about that..."` 来对他的时间安排提出疑问？

5.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question):**
    *   Imagine Tom accepts your points. Now, you need to finalize the plan. How would you use `"Just to be absolutely clear..."` to summarize the final decisions on the discount and the announcement date?
    *   想象一下Tom接受了你的观点。现在，你需要最终确定计划。你会如何使用 `"Just to be absolutely clear..."` 来总结关于折扣和宣布日期的最终决定？
