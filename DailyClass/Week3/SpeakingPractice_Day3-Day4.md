
# 口语练习 (Speaking Practice) - Day 3 & 4

## 话题 (Topic)
Pitching a new product and giving/receiving feedback.
(推销新产品与给出/接收反馈)

---

## 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)
*   **pitch**: 推销，销售演示
*   **track record**: 业绩记录
*   **key strengths**: 主要优势
*   **feedback**: 反馈
*   **concerned**: 担心的，关切的
*   **telephone manner**: 电话礼仪
*   **work on**: 努力改善，致力于
*   **I'm a little bit concerned about...**: 我对...有点担心
*   **You need to work on...**: 你需要改进...
*   **Perhaps you should think about...**: 也许你应该考虑...

---

## 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

1.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   Have you ever had to "pitch" an idea or product to someone? How did it go?
    *   (你有没有向别人推销过一个想法或产品？结果如何？)

2.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   How do you usually give feedback to others, especially when it's negative? What's your approach?
    *   (你通常如何给别人反馈，特别是负面反馈？你的方法是什么？)

3.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   Imagine you are pitching a new, innovative service to a potential client. What would you say are its `key strengths`? How would you highlight your company's `track record` to build trust?
    *   (想象一下你正在向一个潜在客户推销一项创新服务。你会说它的主要优势是什么？你会如何强调你公司的业绩记录来建立信任？)

4.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   Your colleague's `telephone manner` is not very professional, and you are `concerned` about it. How would you politely tell them `You need to work on...` their phone etiquette, using the expressions we learned?
    *   (你同事的电话礼仪不太专业，你对此感到担忧。你会如何礼貌地告诉他们需要改进电话礼仪，使用我们学过的表达？)

5.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Let's role-play. One person is a manager giving feedback to an employee about their `pitch` skills. The other person is the employee receiving the feedback. Use phrases like `I'm a little bit concerned about...`, `You need to work on...`, and `Perhaps you should think about...`.
    *   (我们来角色扮演。一个人是经理，给员工的推销技巧提供反馈。另一个人是员工，接收反馈。使用 `I'm a little bit concerned about...`, `You need to work on...`, 和 `Perhaps you should think about...` 等短语。)

6.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Why is it important to give feedback in a polite and constructive way, even when you are `concerned` about something? How does it impact the recipient?
    *   (为什么即使你对某事感到担忧，也要以礼貌和建设性的方式给出反馈？这会如何影响接收者？)
