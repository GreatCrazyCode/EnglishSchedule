
# Day4-15: TELEPHONE TELL-TALE (电话里的秘密)

## 1. 学习资料 (Link to Material)
*   [15_TELEPHONE TELL-TALE](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/english-at-work/14-giving-feedback)

## 2. 核心词汇 (Useful Vocabulary)
1.  **feedback** (n.) - /ˈfiːdbæk/
    *   中文解释: 反馈意见
2.  **concerned** (adj.) - /kənˈsɜːnd/
    *   中文解释: 担心的，关切的
3.  **telephone manner** (n.) - /ˈtelɪfəʊn ˈmænə(r)/
    *   中文解释: 电话礼仪
4.  **work on** (phrasal verb) - /wɜːk ɒn/
    *   中文解释: 致力于，努力改善
5.  **polite** (adj.) - /pəˈlaɪt/
    *   中文解释: 有礼貌的
6.  **have a word** (phrase) - /hæv ə wɜːd/
    *   中文解释: （与某人）谈一谈，简短交谈

## 3. 关键句型 (Key Expressions)
1.  **I'm a little bit concerned about...**
    *   中文: 我对...有点担心。
    *   应用场景: 一种委婉地提出问题或表达担忧的方式，比直接批评更礼貌。
    *   例句: "I'm a little bit concerned about the project's deadline. Do you think we can finish it on time?" (我有点担心项目的截止日期。你觉得我们能按时完成吗？)
2.  **You need to work on...**
    *   中文: 你需要改进/加强...
    *   应用场景: 在给出反馈时，明确指出对方需要提升的方面。
    *   例句: "Your ideas are creative, but you need to work on your presentation skills to make them more convincing." (你的想法很有创意，但你需要提升你的演讲技巧，让它们更有说服力。)
3.  **Perhaps you should think about...**
    *   中文: 也许你应该考虑...
    *   应用场景: 一种非常温和、间接的提建议的方式，给对方留出空间和尊重。
    *   例句: "Perhaps you should think about double-checking the data before sending the report." (也许你在发送报告前应该考虑再检查一遍数据。)

## 4. 随堂练习 (Exercises)
1.  **填空题 (Fill in the blank):**
    *   His ______ is not very professional; he often sounds impatient on the phone.
    *   (他的______不太专业；他在电话里听起来常常很不耐烦。)
    *   **Answer**: telephone manner

2.  **情景问答 (Situational Question):**
    *   You are a manager and you need to tell your employee that their reports are often late. How could you use "I'm a little bit concerned about..." to start the conversation politely?
    *   (你是一名经理，你需要告诉你的员工他的报告经常迟交。你会如何使用 “I'm a little bit concerned about...” 来礼貌地开始这次谈话？)
    *   **Example Answer**: "Hi [Employee's Name], could I have a word? I'm a little bit concerned about the submission schedule for your recent reports. Let's talk about how we can ensure they are on time." (你好[员工姓名]，我能和你说句话吗？我有点担心你最近报告的提交时间。我们来谈谈如何确保它们能准时提交吧。)
