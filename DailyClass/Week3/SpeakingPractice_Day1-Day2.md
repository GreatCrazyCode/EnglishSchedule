
# 口语练习 (Speaking Practice) - Day 1 & 2

## 话题 (Topic)
Preparing for an important presentation and managing workload.
(准备重要报告与管理工作量)

---

## 核心词汇 (Key Vocabulary)
*   **overtime**: 加班
*   **burn the candle at both ends**: 过度劳累，拼命工作
*   **burn out**: (因工作过度而)筋疲力尽
*   **put in the hours**: 投入大量时间
*   **presentation**: 演示，报告
*   **mess up**: 搞砸
*   **I'll start by...**: 我会先从...开始
*   **Then I'm going to move on to discuss...**: 接着，我会继续讨论...

---

## 练习问题 (Practice Questions)

1.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   Do you often work overtime? Why or why not?
    *   (你经常加班吗？为什么？)

2.  **热身问题 (Warm-up Question)**
    *   Have you ever given a presentation? How did you feel before it started?
    *   (你做过报告吗？在开始前你感觉如何？)

3.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   Imagine you have a big presentation next week. How many hours would you put in to prepare for it? Do you think it's necessary to burn the candle at both ends?
    *   (想象一下你下周有一个重要的报告。你会投入多少小时来准备？你认为有必要为此废寝忘食吗？)

4.  **核心问题 (Core Question)**
    *   If you see a colleague working too much overtime, you might worry they will burn out. How would you express your concern to them using the phrases we've learned?
    *   (如果你看到一位同事加班太多，你可能会担心他们会累垮。你会如何用我们学过的短语来表达你的关心？)

5.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   Let's role-play. You are about to start a presentation about "The Importance of Work-Life Balance". Use the phrases `Today I'm going to...`, `I'll start by...`, and `Then I'm going to move on to...` to give your opening.
    *   (我们来角色扮演一下。你即将开始一个关于“工作与生活平衡的重要性”的报告。请使用 `Today I'm going to...`, `I'll start by...`, 和 `Then I'm going to move on to...` 来做开场白。)

6.  **扩展问题 (Extension Question)**
    *   What is the biggest risk of putting in too many hours on a single project? Do you think it increases the chance to mess up in the end?
    *   (在单个项目上投入过多时间最大的风险是什么？你认为这最终会增加搞砸的几率吗？)
